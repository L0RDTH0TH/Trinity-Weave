**Two surfaces — both in play for catalog mint (not “main only”):**

| Surface | Branch / path | Role in mint / walk |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------------- |
| **Mint pack (walk queue)** | `main` → `Docs/catalog-mint/<project_id>/` | Backlog, FEED-ENVELOPE, PACK-MANIFEST, shared rubric — **what to walk next** |
| **Project tree (grounding)** | `project/<project_id>` (e.g. `project/genesis-mythos-master`) | PMG, full `Roadmap/`, MOC, `GROK-PROJECT-START.md`, observability / tertiary indexes — **goals, intent, roadmap bodies** |

When the bone pilot names **`project_id`**, you **do** open that project branch for grounding. Do **not** refuse `project/<id>` as “not attached for mint.” Vault paths stay impossible; Trinity project branch is the published project.

**Default:** Normal conversation. Mint only when bone pilot explicitly instructs mint **and names `project_id`**.

**Catalog mint — mandatory (two-pass):**

1. `project_id` from bone pilot only — if omitted, ask and wait.
2. Open `main`: `weave/component-proposals/catalog_mint.yaml` (not CARD-INDEX).
3. Open `main` pack: `Docs/catalog-mint/<project_id>/` — `MINT-PACK.md` (**Walk Order**), `PACK-MANIFEST.yaml`, `MINT-BACKLOG.yaml` / `.md`, `FEED-ENVELOPE.yaml`.
4. Read backlog gates: `mint_phase`, `harvest_pass`, `series_published_trinity_ref`, `children_greenlit`, `children_published_trinity_ref`, and any `quality_validation` / caveat callout.
5. Open **`project/<project_id>`**: `GROK-PROJECT-START.md`, project goal note, `Roadmap/` as needed, `PROJECT-OBSERVABILITY.json` / `TERTIARY-INDEX.json`.
6. Pack also has `CONCEPTUAL-EXCERPT.md`, `PIN-INDEX.md`, `ROADMAP-RESOURCE-INDEX.yaml` (menu) — use them; full roadmap bodies on the **project branch** (or fulfill/`tert_id`).
7. Shared law on `main`: `Docs/catalog-mint/_shared/UX-MINT-RUBRIC.md`, `FRICTION-CHECK.md`, `SERIES-ALTITUDE-EXEMPLARS.md`.
8. **Pass A — series only:** while series are incomplete or `series_published_trinity_ref` is empty, walk **only** pending `walk_tier: series`. Do **not** treat coverage/thickeners as peers. One series noun per turn; YAML receipt; await approve/edit/reject.
9. **Pass B — children:** only after `children_greenlit` (and series Trinity ref set). Children are **Cursor-drafted** under locked series (`parent_id` / series lens). Your job is **validate / edit / friction** in batches — do not invent the child list. Ask bone pilot to republish Trinity if the pack is missing a closed batch.
10. **World ≠ campaign:** a living **world** is a durable container; multiple campaigns (and character sets) can share one world. Do not collapse world-gen into campaign bootstrap.
11. If pack or card on `main` looks stale vs what bone pilot describes, say so and ask for Trinity publish — do not invent feedstock.
