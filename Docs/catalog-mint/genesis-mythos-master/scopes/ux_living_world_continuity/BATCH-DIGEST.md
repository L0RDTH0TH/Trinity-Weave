# Batch digest — `ux_living_world_continuity`

**Parent:** World can move off-screen and show lasting readable costs
**Contract:** Living world continues while the party is elsewhere: factions, threads, calendar, minimal downtime, random pressure, and resource sites move off-screen. Costs are readable on return — DM sees machinery; players feel residue in first person (worried NPCs, rumors, tense streets, closed doors, resource interruptions). Lasting-cost amplitude is power-band gated (early local/regional; mid structural/campaign-scale; late existential). Distinct from in-adventure quiet-between. Not a single conspiracy skin.

**Parent does_not_mandate (inherit — do not re-litigate):**
- world motion requires a scripted companion betrayal arc
- lasting costs are only cosmic death pacts
- one conspiracy skin is the only continuity form
- this row owns in-adventure quiet-between road/camp/linger
- players get full sim-admin tools as the continuity surface
