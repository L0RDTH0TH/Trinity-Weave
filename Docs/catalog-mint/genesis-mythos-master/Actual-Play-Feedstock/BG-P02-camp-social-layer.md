# Felt moment (pattern language)

Between adventuring days, the product becomes a **camp**: firelight, companion conversations, approvals, jokes, confessions. Identity and class fantasy show up as talk and presence — not a character sheet modal first. Quiet between pillars is intentional UX, not dead air. Digital analog of table downtime.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Intimate staging; player chooses who to approach. No initiative tracker. Soft framing via companion initiation. Control is social initiative and when to sleep forward.
