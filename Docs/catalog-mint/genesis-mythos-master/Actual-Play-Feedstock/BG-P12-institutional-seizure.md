# Felt moment (pattern language)

A soft-power institution (wizards’ guild, city law, church) **takes someone you travel with**. Not a random death — a legal or arcane abduction. The party fractures into rescue planning, resentment, and world-systems learning. Models institutional soft framing: you cannot always swing first.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Public street → custody. Attention on the missing seat. Control is bureaucratic and investigative before combat.

## Experience noun candidates

- label: Soft-power party seizure
  summary: A living companion removed by law/magic, not HP.
- label: Rescue as social contract
  summary: The table re-forms around getting them back.
