# Felt moment (pattern language)

BG3-class digital feel still allows authored spaces; GMM’s difference is **who operates the story**. Consequence must be able to come from a human DM, a living-world sim, or table negotiation — not only from a pre-baked dialogue wheel with three painted outcomes.

Harvest and mint must refuse collapsing “social agency” into “pick A/B/C.” Option trees may exist as *tools*, never as the sole model of social texture. The world remembering (combat cast, camp trust, faction stance) is the digital proof; the human operator is the table proof.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Social beats leave room for free speech / DM soft framing / sim reaction. Soft framing suggests stakes without locking the verb list to three buttons. Control includes inventing a fourth move.
