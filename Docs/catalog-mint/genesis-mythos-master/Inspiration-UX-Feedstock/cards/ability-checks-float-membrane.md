# Ability Checks — float membrane

**Job:** ability checks are the **primary resolution membrane** between character capability and the living world. They resolve uncertainty in a way that feels like the character’s actual body, mind, or training pressing against the world.

## Float model

Information is a **continuous trickle** rather than binary on/off.

- Level of detail scales with the relevant ability score and is amplified by proficiency.
- Only scores of **6 and under** produce no float (“too dumb for it”).
- Abnormally high scores (soft guidance: well above normal playable range) yield richer, more precise, or earlier information even without proficiency.
- Each score floats information in its natural domains (INT → analysis / lore / investigation, WIS → insight / perception / survival, etc.).

## Player-facing result

Floated information appears as character knowledge the player can see and remember. The character “just knows” or notices.

## DM Information Gate (recurring control surface)

When the system determines that certain knowledge is reasonable to surface given score, proficiency, and situation, it **proposes** the information. The DM may **pass, partially pass, delay, or block** it. The membrane is permeable by the system but never fully automatic. Party actions can still force leaks through ordinary world interaction.

(Same gate already used for treasure provenance flags and BBEG / apex-node information control.)

## Check still matters

Floating knowledge improves what the character perceives or recalls going into a situation. It does **not** auto-succeed contested, uncertain, or actively opposed actions.

## Relation to locked surfaces

Directly extends the investment-chrome pattern from Monsters & behaviors (cohesion / push-to-run), Traps (discoverability), and Hirelings (loyalty visibility). Backgrounds and knowledge-flow channels become stronger when paired with high relevant scores. Conditions that impair scores temporarily suppress the corresponding floats. Session prep can surface currently active or gated floats for DM awareness.

**Shared magic / environment readability (2026-08-14):** high relevant proficiency may float recognition of persistent magical terrain, concentration, zones, or residual magic weather as felt conditions — see [[conditions-felt-state-machine]]. No new physics system.

## Refuse (required)

- Only proficiency unlocks knowledge (extreme talent must also matter)
- Binary float with no detail scaling
- Fully automatic revelation with no DM gate
- High score auto-succeeding every related check
- Universal float of unrelated domains just because one score is high
