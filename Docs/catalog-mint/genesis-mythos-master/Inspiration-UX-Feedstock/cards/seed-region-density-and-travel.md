# Seed-region density and travel

Hard funnel on seed town/city + hinterland; soft openness beyond. Logistics continuous; magic (not mundane) for speed; DM frame-jump allowed when fair.

## One party per campaign

Not an MMO. Negotiation about where to go / what to do next stays inside the party (+ DM). No multi-party contention over the same quest or caravan slot. Destiny refuse = fireteam size cap only.

## Economic substrate

Blacksmith needs inputs; stores need flows; approximate population / economic strength known or generatable — realism under A/B/C labor textures (`intent-shape-to-realization`).

## Refuse (required)

- Mundane fast travel as default
- Checklist open-world density
- Logistics that only exist at high band
- Multi-party seed / contention protocol
