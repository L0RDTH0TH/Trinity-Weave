# Quiet travel / camp loop

**Job:** the classic open-world travel and camping loop is the ordinary surface where exploration occurs.

## Pattern

Quiet-between is **not** a separate explore mode. It is continuous presence in the same world.

Concrete rhythm:

1. Party leaves a place (town, camp, site).
2. Moves through the day: travel, stop for lunch / care for pack animals or mounts, continue.
3. Sets camp, establishes watch rotation, sleeps.
4. Wakes and repeats until destination — or until the table decides to linger, divert, or engage something.

Weather follows the calendar / living world. The camping / travel rhythm **is** the texture. Quiet is the low-intensity frame.

## Refuse (required)

- BG3 campsite companion UI as the quiet skin
- Long-rest refill as the only job
- Loading state between pillars
- Separate explore-mode swap
- Mundane fast travel as the ordinary surface
