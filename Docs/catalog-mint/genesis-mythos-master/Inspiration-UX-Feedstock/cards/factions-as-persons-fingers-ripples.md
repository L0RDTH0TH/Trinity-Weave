# Factions as persons — fingers and ripples

**Job:** treat a faction as a **person with a body**, not a meter and not a Clanfolk clone of every pocket.

## Pattern

Factions have **needs** (food, shelter, security, succession, reputation, resources). They **occupy places**. They act through **fingers** — specific NPCs, patrols, merchants, messengers, work crews, shrines, ledgers.

Players rarely meet the whole body. They meet the fingers and feel the **ripples**.

**Stones in the water:** the faction’s current goal or crisis produces local effects the party can notice and react to while simply existing in the place.

**Players can become fingers or stones.** Hired, courted, blamed, protected, or used. Same surface. Class desks only thicken *how* a particular character performs inside the interaction.

## Altitude

| Layer | Feel |
|-------|------|
| High | Regional / institutional heartbeat |
| Pocket | The specific household / guild / village currently being touched |

Same faction can carry both. Players usually feel the pocket first. Pocket does **not** replace high.

## Hand-off

Class-expression desks own character-specific social expression (Bard motif, Warlock ledger, Paladin oath, …). This surface is the shared institutional weather whether or not a class desk is open.

## Refuse (required)

- Every faction is a Clanfolk household clone
- Pocket replaces high
- Must meet the whole body / open a faction sheet
- Class desk required to meet a finger
- Bannerlord campaign-map skin as product default
