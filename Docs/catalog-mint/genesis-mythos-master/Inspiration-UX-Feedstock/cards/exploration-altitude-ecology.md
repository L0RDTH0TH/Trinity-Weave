# Exploration altitude — ecology nest

High region/biome continuity · pocket site micro-ecology · settlement threat-pressure gradient.

## Settlement threat pressure

Playable-race settlements radiate threat-level pressure that pushes stronger monsters outward. Pressure bleeds into surrounding weaker settlements → readable gradient. Safe-ish seed zones without nerfing whole ecology. Ties to `critical-node-hierarchy` + `seed-region-density-and-travel`.

## Loop shape

Locate → read land/habitat → confront (not mandatory lethal finish). Apex presence = event.
