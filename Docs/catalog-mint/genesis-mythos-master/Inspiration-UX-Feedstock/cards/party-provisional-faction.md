# Party as provisional faction

**Job:** hold the party as both **free-willed individuals** and a **single provisional faction** under a gentlemen’s agreement of common goals.

## Dual nature

| Layer | Texture |
|-------|---------|
| Individuals | Free will, digression, dissent, private secrecy contests |
| Provisional faction | Shared surface the living world can address as one actor |

Not a rigid hive-mind. Not a pure collection of solos with no shared institutional address.

## How the world treats the party

Larger factions may treat the party as **finger**, **stone**, or **target** — same grammar as institutional weather.

Internal pressure through **one member** is already possible via existing faction fingers. That is **illustration only** — not a new party-politics desk.

## Hirelings

Hirelings are recorded as **fingers of the party’s faction**: subordinate to the core party, but explicitly the party-faction’s own fingers (not a second party).
