# Multiclass / feats / post-20 — same character

**Job:** multiclass, feats, and post-20 capacity expand what a character can do while remaining **readable as the same character’s expression surface**. They must not create a second class desk or replace the primary identity.

## Hard constraints (already locked)

- Thin-base capacity philosophy remains.
- Subclass-primary expression remains.
- Fixed primary direction remains.
- Core class + subclass stay the **dominant silhouette and capacity source**.

## Multiclass

Relatively open, **DM-gated**. The system does not hard-block combinations; the DM is the limit. Soft guidance: approval should consider thematic resonance and resulting readability so the character still presents a coherent silhouette to the table and the living world.

## Feats

Mechanical **permissions** that let the player move through the world in differing ways. Valid domains include combat style, social-permeable membrane access, exploration approaches, craft, institutional levers, and similar. Not limited to pure combat sharpeners. Feats sharpen or extend a clear aspect of the character rather than functioning as a separate shopping-menu game.

## Post-20 / high-band boons

Also **DM-gated** on the same pattern (player request + DM approval). Continues the same character and the same living-world pressures at higher altitude. Soft thematic and readability guidance applies. Not a disconnected “epic tier” with its own unrelated menu.

## Transformations (clarified)

Transformations are **leveled and progressive**. They are **not** restricted to A-band. A character in C-band may access and advance through the stages appropriate to their current power and reach; progression can continue through B, A, and beyond. Transformations are an orthogonal identity path that intersects power bands rather than sitting only at the top of them. (Full transformative anchors and sibling structure already locked under Conditions.)

## World readability

Expansions should leave the character **legible**. Other characters, NPCs, and the living world should still be able to read what this person is. Soft guidance for DM approval emphasizes coherent silhouette and thematic resonance.

## Power-band awareness

Sideways and upward growth still respects the existing C → B → A reach and residual-pressure logic. Transformations follow their own staged progression within that framework.

## Refuse (required)

- Feat shopping as the primary character-progression game
- Multiclass results that read as two classes glued together with no coherent identity
- Post-20 epic-boon menu disconnected from prior character and living world
- Any expansion that re-opens or violates thin-base, subclass-primary, or fixed primary direction
- Treating transformations as A-band-only content
