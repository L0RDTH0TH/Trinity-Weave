## Primary labor exemplar (closed 2026-08-13)

**Lock:** realism-with-optional-traceability.

| Rule | Texture |
|------|---------|
| Continuous | Labor and material flows are real and continuous (background economy already locked). |
| Presence | Only renders current state. |
| Optional depth | When a player cares, they may follow caravans, production sites, scarcity signals, and ownership chains to source. |
| Refuse as primary | No mandatory tracking, no factory sim, no place-build loop. |
