# Class-expression board — Cursor draft v4

**Role:** Player-facing texture for `class-unique-expression-surfaces`. Pattern language only. **Not locked** — Grok+User validate. Pin gate remains **open**. Not applied seasoning.

**Scope:** SRD 5.1 major classes + Artificer. Shared surfaces: Tools, Languages, Inventory (unchanged stubs/desk).

**This pass (v4):** density — every subclass-primary desk at Sense → Play → Outcome grain; thin base precursors flavored (still secondary, one sentence). Structure from v3 stands.

**Leave untouched:** Tools / Languages stubs, economy, full magic-item desk.

## Artificer

**Primary direction (fixed):** Bind weave into objects that must survive the world.  
**Thin base precursor:** The first object that holds a rule without falling apart. Grows slowly with level + any coherent making/stress. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Gadgeteer — invent, tune, field-test.  
**Pattern taught:** Network / infrastructure / tolerance under load.  
**Refuse:** Factory-sim wholesale; craft wall; potion-sim clone; “not a wizard.”

### Subclass-primary desk — commission / material / role chain

The **base piece** is the object. Same chain, different material.

1. **Sense:** Commission brief — role the piece must play; materials on hand; where it will fail first.
2. **Play:** Source → shape → bind weave → field-test under the role’s stress. Hands: commit materials, set tolerances, run the test, scrap or lock.
3. **Outcome:** Piece holds its role **or** readable failure. Manual = on-theme, lower scrap. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (misfire).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Alchemist** — dose / reaction; reagents; timing + effect; failure = bad batch.
   - **Artillerist** — field engine; housing + charge; placement + calibration.
   - **Battle Smith** — companion construct; frame + loadout; survive beside you (continuous; skip/murderhobo apply).
   - **Armorer** — suit; plates; mode integrity on a body.
5. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal. World/NPC artificers still make things.
6. **Soft-teaching:** The bench test *is* the lesson.

## Barbarian

**Primary direction (fixed):** Ancestral / inner-storm contact.  
**Thin base precursor:** The storm answers a shout — a generic stoke/cool after coherent violence. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Force of nature who *becomes* the fight.  
**Pattern taught:** Charge / recovery / overheat inside live inner weather.  
**Refuse:** Rage as only a combat toggle; “dumb brute” as the whole identity.

### Totem / Wild Heart — spirit-path journey

1. **Sense:** Which spirit-path is open after recent stress. Totem pressure as weather — animal presence, not a menu pet.
2. **Play:** Walk a short path (offer, follow, bargain, refuse). Hands: path token, endure a beat, take or reject.
3. **Outcome:** Seated totem posture **or** refused/sour path. Manual = on-theme spirit. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (wrong spirit, debt).
4. **Refuse:** Spirit as fashion; culturally lazy animal-picker.
5. **Soft-teaching:** The bargain’s return *is* the lesson.

### Berserker — wave / mental arena

1. **Sense:** Next wave’s height after recent stress. Arena = inner space, not a second overworld.
2. **Play:** Ride or cut the wave (hold, break, redirect). Hands: commit, bleed heat, crash vs channel.
3. **Outcome:** Usable next-fight posture **or** leftover arena noise. Manual = cleaner ride. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (you come out wrong).
4. **Refuse:** QTE-only; “mindless” as the only fantasy.
5. **Soft-teaching:** How the wave landed *is* the lesson.

### Ancestral Guardian — chorus journey (full grain)

Same storm; the path is a **chorus**, not an animal.

1. **Sense:** Which ancestors are leaning in after recent stress — who is loud, who is silent, who is owed. Chorus weather: protective, accusing, hungry for witness.
2. **Play:** Call, placate, or refuse a short chorus beat. Hands: name a voice, offer a memory or a stand, accept or turn aside the ask. Primary stays inner-storm contact; secondary may be social (the living hear the dead).
3. **Outcome:** A seated guardian posture (who stands with you next fight) **or** a sour chorus (voices that will not leave, or will not come). Manual = coherent ancestors, cleaner ask. Skip = near-average chorus; rare real-bad (wrong dead, lingering claim).
4. **Soft-teaching:** Which voice answered — and what it cost — *is* the lesson.
5. **Refuse:** Ancestor as buff icon; culturally lazy “generic tribal dead.”

### Zealot — vow-brand / pain-wave (full grain)

Same storm; the wave’s material is **sacred pain / vow**.

1. **Sense:** Where the last vow burned or cooled after recent stress — brand heat, sacred debt, a pain that wants a shape.
2. **Play:** Meet the pain-wave in a short arena (brand, hold, dedicate). Hands: choose the vow to feed, ride the pain, seal or abort the brand.
3. **Outcome:** A usable zealot posture (next fight reads as dedicated) **or** a brand that soured (pain without meaning, vow that now owns you). Manual = on-theme dedication, lower leftover. Skip = near-average brand; rare real-bad (hollow zeal, wound that will not close into a vow).
4. **Soft-teaching:** Whether the pain became a vow *is* the lesson.
5. **Refuse:** Torture-porn as identity; zeal as only a damage buff.

## Bard

**Primary direction (fixed):** Shape rooms with performance.  
**Thin base precursor:** Rooms begin to lean toward the voice — generic heat that grows slowly with level + any coherent performance or social stress. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Social physicist. Words, songs, and scenes bend rooms; the party’s spotlight director.  
**Pattern taught:** Audience / feedback loops — read a room, adjust signal, bank what lands.  
**Refuse:** Mandatory karaoke; endless dialogue trees as the only loop; “horny lute guy” flatten; desk as a second combat rotation.

### Subclass-primary desk — motif / repertoire

The **room** is the object; the **motif** is the tool. Same loop, different material.

1. **Sense:** What the room (or the people in it) is already doing after recent stress or quiet. Audience heat as readable weather: open, guarded, restless, hungry for a flavor, already claimed by someone else’s performance. Higher level = subtler tells (who is performing status, who is performing fear, which silence is a held breath). Subclass tints the tells.
2. **Play:** Compose or pull a short motif → place it into the room → read the immediate return. Hands: beats / emotion tags / length, cut or extend, bank or spend now. Downtime / between-scenes. Combat yields it. Secondary lean may be social consequence or light exploration (the room remembers); primary never leaves “shape the room with performance.”
3. **Outcome:** Banked heat (later social leverage, scene control, or a readable change in how the room treats the party) **or** a dropped / sour motif that makes the next entry colder or more expensive. Manual = on-theme, coherent, cheaper. Skip / murderhobo = near-average stock motif; rare real-bad (wrong room, public misfire, heat that belongs to someone else now).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Lore** — cutting-word / knowledge-riff library. Sense the gap in what the room knows or will admit. Play the precise cut or well-placed fact. Outcome: a room suddenly more honest, or more defensive.
   - **Valor / Swords** — martial flourish choreography. Sense readiness for displayed competence or challenge. Play the short form that turns violence-adjacent performance into social fact. Outcome: respect, challenge accepted, or the room decides you are only theater.
   - **Glamour** — courtly / dream-audience. Sense the surface the room wants to believe. Play the glamour that lets them live in it for a moment. Outcome: temporary consent, borrowed status, or the dream cracking when the light hits it.
   - **Whispers** — secret / fear motif bank. Sense the fracture or the unnamed thing. Play the quiet motif that makes the fracture legible (or deeper). Outcome: leverage, a room that no longer trusts its silence, or blowback when the wrong person hears.
   - **Creation** — temporary prop / set-piece. Sense what physical or sensory object would change the room’s geometry. Play the short conjured set-piece. Outcome: a stage, barrier, or witness that was not there — lifespan is part of the cost.
5. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal. Murderhobo: no motif desk; rooms and NPCs still respond to performance in the world.
6. **Soft-teaching:** The heat return *is* the lesson — which beat landed, which dropped the room, which silence was a held breath.

## Cleric

**Primary direction (fixed):** Serve the living channel / tradition / domain practice.  
**Thin base precursor:** The first omen that arrives without being asked. Grows slowly with level + any coherent rite/stress. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Living radio to a god; faith with paperwork and miracles.  
**Account:** You **serve** the channel. Institutions optional (may share temples with Paladins).  
**Pattern taught:** Protocol / tradition maintenance.  
**Refuse:** Spell menu only; “Cleric has church, Paladin doesn’t”; mandatory mass as a gate.

### Subclass-primary desk — domain rite / flock

Continuous flock/rite **is** this desk.

1. **Sense:** Omen and flock weather after recent stress — blurry vs clear, who is hungry, which offering is stale, which silence in the parish is fear. Higher level = subtler domain tells. Subclass tints what you can hear.
2. **Play:** Prepare the rite grid (offerings, hours, domain tags) → commit the channel → read clarity. Hands: slot prayers, weigh offerings, clear corruption, tend one flock beat. Downtime. Combat yields it. Primary never leaves “serve the channel.”
3. **Outcome:** Channel clarity + flock/parish state **or** sour omen / neglected flock. Manual = on-theme domain, lower tithe cost. Skip = near-average rite; rare real-bad (omen sour, flock slip).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Life** — vitality ledger. Sense who is failing in the flock. Play the rite that restores or ration-heals. Outcome: a parish that holds, or one that has begun to die quietly.
   - **War** — banner rites. Sense the fight the flock is already in (or about to be). Play the bless / standard. Outcome: a room that will stand, or a banner that now demands blood.
   - **Knowledge** — scriptorium / omen catalog. Sense the gap in what the tradition remembers. Play the cataloging rite. Outcome: a usable omen-file, or a fact that did not want to be written.
   - **Trickery** — mask / misdirection rites. Sense which face the channel needs you to wear. Play the mask. Outcome: a useful lie in service of the god, or a mask that will not come off.
   - **Nature** — seasonal / rural parish. Sense the season the land and flock share. Play the rural rite. Outcome: harvest/weather alignment, or a season that turns on the parish.
   - **Death / Grave** — boundary / funeral. Sense where the dead are leaning on the living. Play the boundary rite. Outcome: a clean crossing, or a debt that now sits on the altar.
5. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal (includes parish). World still runs rites/omens for NPCs.
6. **Soft-teaching:** Blurry vs clear omen *is* the lesson.

## Druid

**Primary direction (fixed):** Nature-aligned always.  
**Thin base precursor:** A patch of land starts answering care. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Gardener of living systems.  
**Pattern taught:** Stewardship / succession.  
**Refuse:** Farm-sim wholesale; chore wall; “tree-hugger” flatten.

### Circle desk — localized terraforming / succession

Land / Spores / Shepherd / Stars / Moon = **material** on the same desk (Moon = which forms the land will accept in you).

1. **Sense:** What the local patch wants next after recent stress. Readable succession: what dies if ignored, what colonizes if helped.
2. **Play:** Plant, prune, flood, burn, inoculate, relocate, open a corridor. Hands: succession tokens on a small patch, one season’s orders. Not a planet editor.
3. **Outcome:** New succession state + boon/affinity **or** blight / runaway colonizer. Manual = on-theme Circle. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (blight).
4. **Subclass material:** Land = plants/biome; Spores = fungi/rot; Moon = form-succession in the body; Stars = sky-garden; Shepherd = flock/spirits.
5. **Soft-teaching:** Wilt / bloom *is* the lesson.

## Fighter

**Primary direction (fixed):** Practice of applied violence + kit.  
**Thin base precursor:** The last fight leaves a readable wear-mark on form and kit. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Professional of violence.  
**Pattern taught:** Deliberate practice + kit systems.  
**Refuse:** “Fighter has no fantasy”; collapsing into Monk; fighting-game clone.

### Subclass-primary desk — timed form + kit

Shared *shape* with Monk; **job** = applied violence + kit (not breath-as-self).

1. **Sense:** Where the last fight wore the **kit** and the **form** — dull edge, bad stance habit, a maneuver that didn’t land. Subclass tints which wear you can read.
2. **Play:** Short timed form strip (stance → window → commitment) **plus** kit swap / spar notes. Hands: hit the windows, change a piece of kit, mark one maneuver. Downtime. Combat yields it.
3. **Outcome:** Refreshed maneuver + kit posture **or** sloppy form leftover. Manual = sharper, lower wear. Skip = near-average stock grade; rare real-bad (kit mismatch).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Champion** — form perfection / crit craft. Sense the sloppy beat. Play the clean strip. Outcome: a form that lands more often, or one that still has a tell.
   - **Battle Master** — maneuver recipe book. Sense which recipe failed in the last fight. Play the drill that rewrites it. Outcome: a usable maneuver card, or one that still costs too much kit.
   - **Eldritch Knight** — weapon + cantrip weave. Sense where steel and cantrip fell out of phase. Play the short weave drill. Outcome: a strike that carries the cantrip, or two separate objects again.
   - **Arcane Archer** — shot-arc / arrow recipes. Sense the arc that missed and why. Play the range strip + arrow prep. Outcome: a readable shot, or an arrow that still lies about distance.
   - **Cavalier** — mount / guard pairs. Sense where rider, mount, or ward-target desynced. Play the paired drill. Outcome: a coherent pair, or a mount/ward that will not take the next order.
5. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal.
6. **Soft-teaching:** Missed window + kit tell *is* the lesson.

## Monk

**Primary direction (fixed):** Body-as-instrument.  
**Thin base precursor:** Breath and beat start arriving as one thing. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Flesh turned into technique.  
**Pattern taught:** Self-regulation / embodied practice.  
**Refuse:** Orientalism-as-product; QTE-only; collapsing into Fighter’s kit desk.

### Subclass-primary desk — timed kata / self-tuning

Shared *shape* with Fighter; **job** = tune the body. Kit is not the subject.

1. **Sense:** Where the **body** desynced — breath, ki cadence, a vow that went cold. Subclass tints the tell.
2. **Play:** Short kata (breath pulses + form ghost). Hands: match the instrument, choose vow emphasis. No loadout minigame. Downtime. Combat yields it.
3. **Outcome:** Banked focus / clean cadence **or** cold body. Manual = cleaner ki, lower strain. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (broken form).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Open Hand** — pure form / redirect. Sense the beat that would not yield. Play the redirect kata. Outcome: a body that turns force, or one that still meets it head-on.
   - **Shadow** — silence / smoke form. Sense where you are still loud. Play the silence kata. Outcome: a form that does not announce itself, or one that still casts a tell.
   - **Four Elements** — elemental overlay. Sense which element the body will currently hold. Play the overlay kata. Outcome: a clean elemental cadence, or an overlay that burns the instrument.
   - **Mercy** — pressure-point heal/harm map. Sense where the map on the body is wrong. Play the point kata. Outcome: a usable heal/harm map, or a point that now hurts you too.
   - **Astral Self** — astral limb calibration. Sense where the extra limbs are out of phase with the flesh. Play the calibration. Outcome: limbs that answer, or limbs that drag.
5. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal.
6. **Soft-teaching:** Desync vs clean cadence *is* the lesson.

## Ranger

**Primary direction (fixed):** Nature-aligned always.  
**Thin base precursor:** A trail starts looking like a sentence. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Edge-walker; maps, marks, bond.  
**Pattern taught:** Tracking / route / bond.  
**Refuse:** Pet-as-gate; iconic-hero flatten; survival-sim homework.

### Beast Master — side-quest hunt + companion care

1. **Sense:** What land and bond need after recent stress — quarry worth a short hunt; companion tell (hunger, injury, distrust, eagerness). Hunt and care are one desk.
2. **Play:** Side-quest-scale hunt (mark → route → strike or release) **and** tend the companion. Hands: marks, short route, care beat. Not a second campaign.
3. **Outcome:** Meat/hide/info + coherent bond **or** sour hunt / neglected companion. Manual = healthier pair. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (lost trust, botched quarry).
4. **Soft-teaching:** Bond tell + missed mark *is* the lesson.

### Hunter — quarry specialization (full grain)

Same hunt-board; object is the **quarry type**, not a companion.

1. **Sense:** What kind of quarry the last stress revealed — armor, flight, pack, undead, etc. Readable specialization weather.
2. **Play:** Mark and route a short hunt tuned to that quarry. Hands: specialization tags, ambush, commit or pass.
3. **Outcome:** Banked quarry-craft (next fight/travel reads that type better) **or** a specialization that now blinds you to other trails. Manual = on-theme hunter. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (wrong quarry read).
4. **Soft-teaching:** Whether the land confirmed the type *is* the lesson.

### Gloom Stalker — first-strike / underdark marks (full grain)

1. **Sense:** Where first light / first noise will betray you after recent stress. Underdark or gloom as weather on the hunt board.
2. **Play:** Place first-strike marks; walk a short dark route; choose when to be seen. Hands: silence tags, first-beat ambush.
3. **Outcome:** A first-strike posture **or** a tell that now owns you in the dark. Manual = cleaner gloom craft. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (you were the noise).
4. **Soft-teaching:** Who announced themselves first *is* the lesson.

### Horizon Walker — planar trail marks (full grain)

1. **Sense:** Where the trail thins into another grammar (gate-scent, wrong stars, local distortion). Same hunt-board; material = planar seam.
2. **Play:** Mark the seam; walk a short route that does not fall through; choose to cross or skirt. Hands: trail tags on the seam.
3. **Outcome:** A usable planar trail **or** a mark that now pulls. Manual = coherent walker craft. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (lost on the wrong side).
4. **Soft-teaching:** Whether the seam read true *is* the lesson.

### Fey Wanderer — bargain / mist marks (full grain)

1. **Sense:** What the mist or the fey ask after recent stress — a bargain weather on the land, not a patron ledger (that is Warlock).
2. **Play:** Mark a mist route; take or refuse a short land-bargain. Hands: mist tags, one gift or one refusal.
3. **Outcome:** A path that holds in fey weather **or** a bargain that now walks with you. Manual = on-theme wanderer. Skip = near-average; rare real-bad (you paid the wrong coin).
4. **Soft-teaching:** What the mist kept *is* the lesson.
5. **Refuse:** Collapsing into Warlock’s contract desk.

## Rogue

**Primary direction (fixed):** Solve via angle + timing.  
**Thin base precursor:** Problems start looking like locks with hinges. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Clean getaway; leverage.  
**Pattern taught:** Constraint satisfaction / risk pathing.  
**Refuse:** Stealth sudoku as a gate; “edgy orphan” flatten.

### Subclass-primary desk — score / angle

The **lock** is the object; the **angle** is the tool.

1. **Sense:** What the problem is already doing after recent stress — entry weather, who is watching, which hinge is real. Higher level = subtler tells. Subclass tints the lock.
2. **Play:** Plan a short score (entry → leverage → exit) → practice one timing beat → bank or run. Hands: place entries, distraction tags, lockpick timing strip. Downtime. Combat yields it. Primary never leaves angle + timing.
3. **Outcome:** Banked angle (later leverage, cleaner entry, a tool that now fits) **or** a blown beat that makes the next score colder. Manual = on-theme, cheaper. Skip = near-average plan; rare real-bad (blown entry, heat on the wrong name).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Thief** — climb / tool / second-story. Sense the vertical or tool hinge. Play the climb/tool beat. Outcome: a path that was not a door, or a tool that announced you.
   - **Assassin** — disguise / strike setup. Sense who the room thinks you are. Play the cover + first-strike setup. Outcome: a name that holds until the cut, or a cover that slips early.
   - **Arcane Trickster** — mage-hand / spell-mark tricks. Sense which lock will answer a cantrip angle. Play the mark/hand beat. Outcome: a cheat that stays quiet, or a spell that was louder than the hinge.
   - **Mastermind** — social leverage / crew board. Sense who can be moved without a lockpick. Play the crew/leverage beat. Outcome: a room that opens from the inside, or a name that now knows too much.
   - **Scout** — wilderness angle / mobile marks. Sense the mobile hinge (trail, camp, patrol). Play the mobile score. Outcome: a path that keeps moving, or tracks that now belong to you.
5. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal.
6. **Soft-teaching:** Which beat blew (noise, time, angle) *is* the lesson.

## Sorcerer

**Primary direction (fixed):** Move *with* the weave; body is the vessel.  
**Thin base precursor:** The vessel holds a current that is not quite a spell yet. Ceiling still grows via level + repeated adventure.

**Fantasy:** Native to body *and* world. Path of least resistance. Contrast Wizard: tames, maps, brutalist.

### Weave-atelier (subclass-seated)

1. **Sense:** Ambient pressure; subclass-tinted **region**; higher level = deeper/subtler.
2. **Play:** At reachable depth, bend/twist live current; read stability and emergence.
3. **Outcome:** Bank, reinforce, or abort. Manual = on-theme. Skip = near-average; *may* hit assigned thematic target; rare real-bad if depth was high.
4. **Subclass leverage:** Native region / bends / vessel feedback / ambient tells — not particle color (Draconic, Wild, Storm, Shadow, Divine Soul, Clockwork).
5. **Cross-class:** May externalize a clean pattern into a scroll for a wizard (transcription consumes the map).
6. **Refuse:** Wizard book clone; skip as gate; vessel as menu unlock.
7. **Soft-teaching:** Stability / blowback *is* the lesson.

## Warlock

**Primary direction (fixed):** Patron ledger.  
**Thin base precursor:** A clause starts ticking when you are quiet. Not the identity desk.

### Subclass-primary desk — patron ledger / debt clock

Continuous debt **is** this desk.

1. **Sense:** Clause pressure after recent stress — which term is due, which favor is warm, which nightmare is an invoice. Higher level = subtler contract tells. Subclass tints the clauses.
2. **Play:** Fulfill, renegotiate, or stall a short pact beat. Hands: accept terms, deliver tokens, rearrange invocation sockets. Downtime. Combat yields it. Primary never leaves the ledger.
3. **Outcome:** Favor spent/gained + invocation state **or** a clause bite / debt spike. Manual = better terms, lower debt. Skip = near-average compliance; rare real-bad (clause bite).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Fiend** — soul-ledger / temptation favors. Sense what the ledger wants taken. Play the delivery. Outcome: paid heat, or a name now on the page.
   - **Archfey** — whim / dream bargains. Sense the current whim. Play the gift or the clever refusal. Outcome: a favor that holds until the mood turns, or a whim that now owns a day.

   - **Hexblade** — weapon-as-patron / oath-edge. Sense what the weapon wants named. Play the edge-vow. Outcome: a weapon that answers, or an edge that now chooses for you.

## Wizard

**Primary direction (fixed):** Tame the weave via external maps into the book.  
**Thin base precursor:** The first clean transcription that survives stress. Grows slowly with level + any coherent study or magical pressure. Not the identity desk.

**Stereotype:** Domineering scholar. Refuses the weave an inch of the self; efficient, brutalist, external.  
**Pattern taught:** External modeling / rule-rewrite — map a system, then impose the map.  
**Refuse:** Spreadsheet-as-fun; copying the Sorcerer atelier; Artificer outside the wizard family; homework walls; skip as a gate.

**Family:** Same weave family as Artificer and Sorcerer. Wizard = map-and-rewrite into the book. Artificer = bind into objects that must survive. Sorcerer = move *with* the live current. Scrolls are maps; wizard transcription **consumes** the map. A sorcerer may optionally externalize a clean pattern into a scroll for a wizard.

### Subclass-primary desk — lab / map / transcription

The **map** is the object; the **book** is the permanent record. External, deliberate, slightly ruthless.

1. **Sense:** What the current map is doing after recent stress or quiet. Field nodes as weather: drift, imbalance, stale reagent, a scroll still “hot” from a sorcerer’s hand, a theory that no longer matches the last fight or ruin. Higher level = subtler tells (which node is lying, which imbalance is a feature, which transcription costs more than the spell). Subclass tints the tells.
2. **Play:** Align field nodes → ink / transcribe / slot reagents → stress-test the copy. Hands: balance nodes, burn the glyph into the book, confirm under load. Downtime. Combat yields it. Secondary lean may be exploratory (the map came from a place) or social (someone else wants the same transcription); primary never leaves “tame and externalize.”
3. **Outcome:** Spell or theory burned into the book (map spent) **or** botched transcription, reagent loss, map that refused to hold. Manual = cleaner maps, lower cost, on-theme school. Skip / murderhobo = near-average stock copy; rare real-bad (warped transcription, reagent that takes something with it, a map that now belongs partly to the weave).
4. **Subclass material:**
   - **Evocation** — raw energy field shapes. Sense the shape force wants and where it will spill. Play the alignment that cages it. Outcome: a clean brutal spell shape, or a map that still wants to explode.
   - **Abjuration** — ward lattices. Sense the weak point or the intrusion already leaning. Play the lattice that holds. Outcome: a solid ward, or one with a known expensive failure mode.
   - **Divination** — omen / portents board. Sense the signal that will not stay put. Play the mapping that forces a readable position (accept the cost of looking). Outcome: a portent you can act on, or one that has started to look back.
   - **Necromancy** — boundary / thrall notes. Sense where living/not is currently soft. Play the transcription that claims or reinforces it. Outcome: usable boundary work, or a note that has begun to write itself. (Stereotype watch: pattern, not cartoon evil.)
   - **Illusion** — sensory falsework. Sense what the room or target already almost believes. Play the falsework that makes the almost a temporary fact. Outcome: clean temporary reality, or one that frays when stressed.
   - **Bladesinging** (martial-scholar) — form + spell weave. Sense where body and map are out of phase. Play the short alignment that lets the spell ride the form. Outcome: a spell that moves with the blade, or one that still feels like a separate object.
5. **Cross-class hook:** May receive a sorcerer’s externalized pattern-as-scroll. Transcription still consumes the map. The sorcerer does not need the map; the wizard does.
6. **Skip / murderhobo:** Universal. Book and world still track what has been successfully externalized.
7. **Soft-teaching:** The unbalanced map *is* the lesson.
