## Job

Quiet-between is the classic open-world **travel and camping loop**. It is where exploration happens as **continuous presence** in the world, not as a separate “explore mode.” Quiet is the low-intensity frame. The camping / travel rhythm **is** the texture.

Quiet is largely the system waiting for the table to move the story.

---

## Locked brief (2026-08-11)

### Concrete rhythm

1. Party leaves a place (town, camp, site).
2. Moves through the day: travel, stop for lunch / care for pack animals or mounts, continue.
3. Sets camp, establishes watch rotation, sleeps.
4. Wakes and repeats until destination — or until the table decides to linger, divert, or engage something.

Weather follows the calendar / living world. Encounters are environmental (terrain, weather, ecology, road conditions) or DM-authored. The system does not force a pillar beat. Institutional weather and class desks remain available inside this loop; they are not suspended.

| Job | Pattern |
|-----|---------|
| **Continuous fiction** | Same world, bodies, and relationships persist. Tone does not reset. Quiet is intentional product, not dead air or a loading state. |
| **Open-world travel / camp loop** | Leave → day travel with practical stops (food, animals/mounts) → camp setup → watch → sleep → wake → repeat. This is the ordinary surface where exploration occurs. |
| **Soft world pressure inside quiet** | Rumor, weather shift, distant sign, NPC or patrol that appears because the world kept moving. Quiet is largely the system waiting for the table to move the story. |
| **Player agency** | Who to talk to, when to sleep, whether to push or linger, what small tasks to take up. No forced next beat. Players may ignore weather or pressure if it is not worth the effort. |
| **Boundary with other surfaces** | Class desks may still be opened inside quiet. Institutional weather continues. Combat exit residue hands into this surface. Quiet does **not** own between-adventures weeks/months calendar downtime (`ux_living_world_continuity` owns that scale). |
