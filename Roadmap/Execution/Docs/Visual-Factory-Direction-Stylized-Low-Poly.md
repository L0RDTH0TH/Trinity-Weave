## What the style is (from the reference)

- **Geometry:** Visible flat facets; soft organic forms reduced to chunky low-poly silhouettes (hat, skulls, trees as assembled planes — not smooth subdivision).
- **Characters / NPCs:** Exaggerated readable proportions (oversized heads, simplified limbs/ribs); toys-with-agency read, not realism.
- **Environment:** “Digital nature” — trunk pillars + clustered leaf polygons; ground as patchwork dirt/grass facets.
- **Color / light:** Saturated, high-contrast local colors; hard facet shading; magic/VFX as crystalline shards and small geometric particles (cubes/diamonds), not soft photographic glow.
- **Texturing:** Minimal; form + flat color carry the look.
