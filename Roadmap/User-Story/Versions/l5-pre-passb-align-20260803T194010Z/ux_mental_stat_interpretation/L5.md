# L5 — `ux_mental_stat_interpretation`

## What it is

INT, WIS, and CHA can drive visual cues on people, places, and objects that hold relevant information. The cue signals that a mental-stat path is available — it does not spill the content. Cues are stat-gated per player where appropriate; the DM can place or fire cues. Structure menu for presentation, not auto-solving the interaction and not one social-scene skin.

## Seat / visibility

**Player-facing affordance first**, with **DM authorship of when cues fire**. Not a DM-only adjudication channel and not a shared “everyone sees the same social temperature UI.” Each player’s INT/WIS/CHA can gate which cues they get; the DM can force or place cues.

## Full vision

- Cue system tied to INT/WIS/CHA and target type
- Per-player visibility by stats; DM override/trigger
- Player chooses to engage the check; cue is invitation, not result
- Rules plugins define stats/check kinds; presentation stays product-side

## Early / PoC cut

One cue channel (e.g. highlight) for one or two check kinds; DM trigger; stat gate. Defer rich multi-color vocab and full social-temperature layering.

## Hard dependencies

Rules surface exposing INT/WIS/CHA and check types; world view that can show a cue on an entity.

## Out of scope

Auto-success narration replacing roll and roleplay; one exemplar insight beat as law; hiding all social play behind scores; full dialogue-tree systems.

## Alternatives not banned

- Subtle diegetic cue vs strong highlight
- Always-on stat aura vs cue only when DM or system arms it
- Shared party cues vs strictly per-player vision
- Sparse cues vs frequent mental-stat texture

## does_not_mandate

- mental stats unlock a single romance dialogue tree
- low mental stats hide all social information
- cues auto-reveal facts without a check or roleplay
- mental-stat cues are DM-only with no player-facing affordance
- one insight beat is the only product presentation
