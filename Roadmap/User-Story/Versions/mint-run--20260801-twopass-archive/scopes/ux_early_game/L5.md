# L5 — `ux_early_game`

## What it is

Early-game is the low power band for the cast: world access, social audience, explore pressure, and combat offer scale to that band. Pillar rhythm follows the band rather than a second axis. Not a tutorial plot, not session prep, and not a mandatory onboarding fantasy. Mid/end stages own their bands the same way — no parallel progression parent.

## Decision (operator)

Keep **power band on the game-stage rows** (early / mid / end). Do **not** split a separate progression series parent. Stages own the band; later coverage can deepen indicators or math.

## Full vision

- Explicit early band as campaign/cast state the system and DM tools can read
- World/NPC access, explore difficulty, combat offer appropriate to the band
- Progression moves the cast within/toward the next band without a scripted first-arc product law
- Prep can author situations inside the band; it does not replace the band contract
- Mid and end stages own their bands the same way

## Early / PoC cut

One early-band flag the session can run under; thin gating of access/offer; defer rich band UI and automated tier tables.

## Hard dependencies

Live play path with world/campaign/character readiness. Not blocked on mid/end rows.

## Out of scope

Session prep authorship/batch; separate progression-series parent; tutorial-only or combat-only early law; shell/main menu; full mid/end band policy.

## Alternatives not banned

- Implicit band (content only) vs visible band indicator
- Strict gates vs soft advice the DM can override
- Fast climb out of early vs long stay in band
- Prep-authored early situations vs mostly emergent within band limits

## does_not_mandate

- first session must teach stolen agency
- early game is combat-only onboarding
- early play is a mandatory tutorial plot
- progression requires a separate series parent from stage bands
- session prep replaces the early power-band contract
