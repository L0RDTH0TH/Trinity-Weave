# L5 — `ux_world_authorship_modability`

## What it is

World change is first-class authorship across the physical container and the esoteric graph (terrain, settlements, eras, factions, populations, high-authority fiction such as Wish). Every structural change gets a computational cost estimate: cheap → in-place; expensive → intentional re-gen with a designed waiting activity (e.g. tournament-style pong or equivalent), then hard-restore — no menu dump. One mod contract covers table/DM and community packages; authority/trust differ, contract shape does not. Microscope-informed non-linear history welcome. Under DM gate; not silent mutation.

## PoC answers (bone pilot)

1. **Waiting activity:** one concrete interim locks the contract (pong-style or simple shared vignette); other activities allowed under the same shape.
2. **Always-queue exclusions:** none hard-coded at series altitude — cost estimate + DM gate decide. Catastrophic graph wipes may still be queued by gate even if an estimate looks cheap.

## Full vision

- Table proposes / DM accepts structural changes to places, factions, eras, populations
- High-authority fiction lands through cost estimate + gate + apply/waiting path
- One mod contract for internal and external packages
- Power band throttles how heavy a change may stick
- Coverage deepens era browsers, mod marketplaces, waiting activities

## Early / PoC cut

One post-create structural change path covering a physical edit and an esoteric edit; thin cost estimate (cheap in-place / expensive waiting then restore); mod contract shape present even if only table/curated packages first. No full public mod store, unrestricted player-lite surgery, or full non-linear timeline editor for players.

## Hard dependencies

World container + campaign frame; collaborative table agency + DM gate; living-world continuity. Not blocked on full backstory or camera depth.

## Out of scope

Mods only after 1.0; timeline editing as unrestricted player-lite default; silent mutation bypassing re-gen/DM gate; menu-dump on expensive re-gen; replacing world-gen or campaign-creation parents; full public mod store + conflict UI in first cut.

## Alternatives not banned

- Thin collaborative seed vs deeper multi-step world authorship sessions
- Heavy Microscope-style era play vs light era tags only
- Curated-only packages vs open community mods later
- Re-gen of a region vs re-gen of a whole biome or faction graph
- Waiting activities other than pong (camp vignette, short challenge, etc.) under the same contract
- Physical-only vs esoteric-only emphasis at different tables

## does_not_mandate

- mods are post-1.0 only
- timeline editing is player-lite default
- world mutation may bypass intentional re-gen and DM gate
- expensive re-gen dumps the table to menus
- physical and esoteric authorship use different cheat paths outside the mod contract
