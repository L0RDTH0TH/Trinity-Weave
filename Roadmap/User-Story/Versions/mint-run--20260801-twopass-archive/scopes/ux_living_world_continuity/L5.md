# L5 — `ux_living_world_continuity`

## What it is

Living world continues while the party is elsewhere: factions, threads, calendar, minimal downtime, random pressure, and resource sites move off-screen. Costs are readable on return — DM sees machinery; players feel residue in first person (worried NPCs, rumors, tense streets, closed doors, resource interruptions). Lasting-cost amplitude is power-band gated (early local/regional; mid structural/campaign-scale; late existential). Distinct from in-adventure quiet-between. Not a single conspiracy skin.

## Band-scaled lasting costs

- **Early:** local/regional texture — rumors, worried NPCs, less-safe roads, faction motion, disrupted resource sites.
- **Mid:** structural/campaign-scale — influence borders, succession, regional war, permanent absences, major trade/resource arteries compromised.
- **Late:** existential pressure on the world container — planar/cosmic scars; high band may include that scale without making death-pacts the only form.

## Boundary vs quiet-between

**This row:** between-adventures downtime, calendar, random pressure, off-screen faction/resource pulse.  
**Not this row:** in-adventure road/camp/linger → `ux_quiet_between_pillars`.

## Full vision

- Off-screen motion weighted by tone, prior play, and power band
- Downtime, calendar, random encounters, resource-site pressure as one living pulse
- DM workbench for agendas/pulses/resources; players feel continuity in FP texture
- Coverage deepens faction/economy/timeline chrome

## Early / PoC cut

One durable off-screen change across a session boundary; minimal faction-agenda pulse; **minimal downtime**; resource interruption as concrete low-band form; thin DM “since you left” + player FP texture; thin calendar/random-encounter hooks. No full multi-faction matrix, cosmic-gate tooling, or deep economy.

## Hard dependencies

World container + campaign frame; collaborative table agency; power-band parents. Not blocked on full camera or combat depth.

## Out of scope

Scripted betrayal as continuity law; lasting costs only as cosmic death pacts; in-adventure quiet ownership; full player sim-admin; one conspiracy/moral-fork skin; full downtime economy in first cut.

## Alternatives not banned

- Sparse vs dense off-screen pulse
- Soft “the world noticed” texture vs hard mechanical scars that block options
- Mostly social/faction residue vs mostly geographic/physical/resource
- Fast-moving political worlds vs slow scar accumulation
- High-band existential threats that are planar/cosmic vs purely political-apocalyptic
- Thin vs richer downtime surfaces later

## does_not_mandate

- world motion requires a scripted companion betrayal arc
- lasting costs are only cosmic death pacts
- one conspiracy skin is the only continuity form
- this row owns in-adventure quiet-between road/camp/linger
- players get full sim-admin tools as the continuity surface
