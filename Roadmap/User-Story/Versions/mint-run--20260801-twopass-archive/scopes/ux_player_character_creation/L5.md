# L5 — `ux_player_character_creation`

## What it is

Player authors and owns a character before and after DM greenlight. Builds may be unbound or against a campaign invite (entry into campaign and world); invited builds disable banned options from world and campaign configs with overwrite-request path. Accept flow: invite → attach → greenlight. Incomplete builds are unfinished characters, not a draft type. Background→world proposals stay DM-gated and retconnable.

## Full vision

- Builder unbound or invite-aware (configs applied early when invited)
- Option-level DM toggles at world and campaign scope
- Overwrite-request path for edge legality
- Greenlight grants play entry; does not reassign ownership
- Identity chrome once in play
- Parallel with world/campaign setup — not forced exit from campaign bootstrap

## Early / PoC cut

Unbound builder plus one campaign-invite path; option-level disable when invited; simple overwrite request; thin dual check at greenlight. Defer rich identity-art and deep toggle matrices.

## Hard dependencies

Rules surface sufficient for option-level pass/fail under two configs. Campaign (and its world) for invite/greenlight — not required to start an unbound character.

## Out of scope

Shared co-author sheet; world-gen and campaign-frame bootstrap; full DM systems workbench beyond configs this surface reads; ownership transfer on accept; trauma/wound product defaults.

## Alternatives not banned

- Unbound build then later invite vs build only into an open invite
- Hard greenlight vs return-with-notes / overwrite negotiation
- One-campaign binding vs later invite into another campaign in the same world
- Minimal builder vs richer chrome after legality is solid
- Aligned vs divergent world/campaign option sets

## does_not_mandate

- characters must start with a stolen-agency wound
- backstory is only flavor text with no play hooks
- ownership transfers to the DM on greenlight
- character creation requires a separate draft object type
- players may only build inside an open campaign invite
