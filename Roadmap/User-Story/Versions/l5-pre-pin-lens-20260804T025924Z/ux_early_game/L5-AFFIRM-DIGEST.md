## Contract (What it is)

Early-game is the low power band for the cast: world access, social audience, explore pressure, and combat offer scale to that band. Pillar rhythm follows the band rather than a second axis. Not a tutorial plot, not session prep, and not a mandatory onboarding fantasy. Mid/end stages own their bands the same way — no parallel progression parent.

- **Seat:** shared_table · **Trigger:** cast is in early power band when opening world access or travel · **Observable response:** reachable places / threats / offers read as low-band scaled · **Refusal/guard:** mid/late offers stay deferred or soft-blocked unless DM overrides · **Residue:** party sees what early can reach without a second progression parent
- **Seat:** shared_table · **Trigger:** social audience or explore pressure fires in early band · **Observable response:** who will talk / what pressure appears matches early stakes · **Refusal/guard:** not a mandatory tutorial plot or stolen-agency onboarding skin · **Residue:** social/explore state stays band-honest for later climb
- **Seat:** shared_table · **Trigger:** combat offer appears while early · **Observable response:** encounter offer and lasting-cost menu scale to early (combat consumes rule math; band gates offer) · **Refusal/guard:** early is not combat-only onboarding · **Residue:** fight outcomes leave early-band-appropriate costs
- **Seat:** privileged_access (DM) · **Trigger:** DM soft-advises or hard-gates a mid-band attempt · **Observable response:** override or block is visible; band policy remains readable · **Refusal/guard:** session prep does not replace the band contract · **Residue:** table knows whether climb was allowed
