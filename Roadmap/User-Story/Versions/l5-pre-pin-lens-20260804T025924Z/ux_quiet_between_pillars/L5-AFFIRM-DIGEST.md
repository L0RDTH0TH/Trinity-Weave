## Contract (What it is)

In-adventure quiet is the product surface for time between pillar bursts — road, camp, linger, and other low-intensity presence — so fiction stays continuous while the table is not in a forced combat, social set-piece, or explore objective. Quiet is largely the system waiting for players and DM to move the story forward. Between-adventures weeks/months sit with living-world continuity, not this parent.

## Moments

- **Seat:** shared_table · **Trigger:** combat / social set-piece / explore objective ends without a forced next pillar · **Observable response:** table enters quiet presence (road/camp/linger chrome); fiction clock keeps running at low intensity · **Refusal/guard:** system must not auto-force the next combat/social/explore beat · **Residue:** party still in the same adventure fiction; no loading wipe
- **Seat:** shared_table (player) · **Trigger:** player moves, camps, or lingers during quiet · **Observable response:** world stays readable and continuous; light authored beat may fire or stay sparse · **Refusal/guard:** quiet is not a loading state; not only a long-rest refill UI · **Residue:** position/time/rest state persists for the next intentional pillar
- **Seat:** privileged_access (DM) · **Trigger:** DM lets quiet continue or arms a light beat / invites a pillar · **Observable response:** DM can keep waiting or offer a soft next beat without stealing agency · **Refusal/guard:** between-adventures weeks/months calendar downtime is not owned here · **Residue:** adventure continuity preserved; living-world owns long gaps
- **Seat:** shared_table · **Trigger:** table chooses rest embedded in quiet vs a separate rest verb · **Observable response:** rest resolves as a quiet affordance (or separate verb) without swapping to campsite companion skin by mandate · **Refusal/guard:** BG3 campsite companion UI is not product law · **Residue:** resources/conditions updated; quiet mode or state remains available
- **Seat:** shared_table · **Trigger:** player or DM starts a combat, social, or explore intent from quiet · **Observable response:** exit quiet into that pillar surface with continuous fiction (no unexplained jump) · **Refusal/guard:** cannot treat quiet as mandatory downtime before every pillar · **Residue:** prior quiet place/time still readable when returning

## PoC cut

PoC proves **exit a pillar → quiet presence (road or camp) → player or DM advances story → re-enter a pillar** without a loading wipe and without auto-forcing the next beat. Defer dense light-beat authoring, rich camp companion chrome, and calendar downtime tools (living-world). Full vision keeps camp-centric and road-centric quiet plus optional sparse authored beats.
