# In-adventure quiet keeps continuous fiction between combat social and explore — complete vision (L5)

> [!note] Pass-B-aligned L5 definition MO

## What it is

In-adventure quiet is the product surface for time between pillar bursts — road, camp, linger, and other low-intensity presence — so fiction stays continuous while the table is not in a forced combat, social set-piece, or explore objective. Quiet is largely the system waiting for players and DM to move the story forward. Between-adventures weeks/months sit with living-world continuity, not this parent.

- **Seat:** shared_table (player) · **Trigger:** player moves, camps, or lingers during quiet · **Observable response:** world stays readable and continuous; light authored beat may fire or stay sparse · **Refusal/guard:** quiet is not a loading state; not only a long-rest refill UI · **Residue:** position/time/rest state persists for the next intentional pillar

## Early / PoC cut

PoC proves **exit a pillar → quiet presence (road or camp) → player or DM advances story → re-enter a pillar** without a loading wipe and without auto-forcing the next beat. Defer dense light-beat authoring, rich camp companion chrome, and calendar downtime tools (living-world). Full vision keeps camp-centric and road-centric quiet plus optional sparse authored beats.
