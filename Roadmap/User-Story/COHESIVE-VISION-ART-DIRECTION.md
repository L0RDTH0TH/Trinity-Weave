## Craft & worldgen experience (north star)

**Townscaper-esque 3D dual-grid procedural craft** is the worldgen *experience* north star: joyful click-add authorship, grid intent that reads as place-painting — **pattern extract, not a Townscaper clone**.

### Step-one authorship model (operator locked 2026-10-01)

Canonical technique cite: [[Townscaper-Dual-Grid-Authorship-YT-Y19Mw5YsgjI-2026-10-01]] — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y19Mw5YsgjI (*How One Guy FIXED Procedural Generation*). Maps how Townscaper achieves its goals; **worldgen step one** model; where DF inspirations spring forth. **Primary pattern card (feedstock locked 2026-10-02):** [[dual-grid-nested-placetile-world-authorship]] (supersedes [[townscaper-click-add-world-element]]).

**Hot Wheels vs real car (must not re-blend):**

| Layer | Metaphor | Under craft cam |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Dual-grid Townscaper craft | Hot Wheels | Tile authorship only — no camera recenter on place; no deforming craft grid into terrain |
| Terrain3D | Real car | Fed by craft intent; consume/inspect under sparky — **not** blended into craft-view tile deformation |

Townscaper **FEEDS** Terrain3D; they stay distinct. Prefer that blends craft tiles with Terrain3D deformation in craft view → refuse / `inspiration_shape_miss`.

| Scale | Feel | Same tooling |
|-------|------|----------------|
| **Open-world dual-grid** | Overworld / landscape craft and placement | Authorship grammar shared |
| **Nested grid (contained spaces)** | Dungeons, interiors, settlements, structures — **handcraftable**, Townscaper-shaped at smaller scale | Same craft UX language at two scales |

**Structures generally = craftable nested grids** — not dungeon-only. Contained spaces are first-class craft surfaces, not a special-case “dungeon mode” that abandons the dual-grid grammar.
