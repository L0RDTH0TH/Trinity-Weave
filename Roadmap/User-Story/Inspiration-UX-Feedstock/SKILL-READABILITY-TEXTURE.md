## Job

Active checks and passive scores are the **same surface**. One side is the deliberate action; the other is the continuous background radar. Both make the current state of the world more legible to the character who has the relevant proficiency or floor, while leaving it opaque or harder-won for those who do not.

---

## Locked brief (2026-08-11)

| Job | Pattern |
|-----|---------|
| **Active skill / tool assist** | Deliberate use (Investigation, Arcana, Nature, thieves’ tools, artisan tools, etc.) can surface a simple low-chrome cue or leave world residue. Examples: Nature outline (green / yellow / red) on animals; Investigation subtle emphasis on the incriminating page or loose board; Arcana read on the type of magic faced. Not a taxonomy HUD. Not required to act. |
| **Passive skill / mental-stat assist** | Continuous floor (Passive Perception, Passive Insight, other mental-stat passives). When the world’s opposing roll or state falls below that floor, the character receives the readability assist automatically. Example: enemy stealth below ranger’s Passive Perception → figure highlighted (or otherwise made legible) in the bush; surprise prevented. No declared check required. Folds into existing mental-stat interpretation work; does not duplicate it. |
| **Tool use as presence** | Attempt leaves residue the table can see or feel (noise, time, traces, altered object). Success/failure is not only a number. |
| **Language as barrier and bridge** | Proficiency changes what can be understood, said, or read. Barriers are real; work-arounds (gesture, intermediary, magic, time) exist and have cost. Not a binary scene lock. |
| **Soft teaching / discovery** | Tools, languages, and the associated readability assists can be learned or revealed through play. No mandatory training desk for every case. |
| **Boundary** | Class desks may thicken *how* a character performs the check or uses the tool/language. Shared surface is ordinary availability + consequence + active/passive assist. Opacity for those without the skill remains intentional. Agency to ignore remains. |

**Nature outline** is a **texture lock** on Combat (active-assist example). It does **not** close the Combat surface or the pin gate.

## Carry-forwards (do not lock from this pass)

**Combat — lock when Combat surface closes:**

> 180° blind-spot arc is the humanoid default, not a universal monster law. Good default for bipedal humanoid-shaped threats; other anatomies / senses get their own readability language (or none) later.

**Combat exit residue — texture lock only** (not pin-gate close):

> The breath the table takes when they realize they are still alive — red border fades, heartbeat slows and softens, heavy breathing fades, combat chrome recedes without a hard cut. DM still owns the official end call.

---

## Explicit refuse list

Do **not**:

1. Make every check only a pass/fail number with no world residue or readability.
2. Turn assist into a mandatory inspect-to-act gate or permanent taxonomy HUD.
3. Make language barrier a hard scene lock with no work-around.
4. Fire passive assists for everyone regardless of score.
5. Require opening a class desk for tools / languages / skill assists to exist.
6. Mandate a training montage for every proficiency.
7. Propose game titles as `conceptual_pin` from this pass.
8. Claim share-ready or close the pin / seasoning gate from this pass.
