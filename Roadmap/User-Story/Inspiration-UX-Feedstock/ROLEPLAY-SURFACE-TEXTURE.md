## Job

Factions are **persons**. They have needs, occupy places, act through fingers. Players rarely meet the whole body; they meet the fingers and feel the ripples. Stones in the water: the faction’s current goal or crisis produces local effects the party can notice and react to while simply existing in the place.

Class desks only thicken *how* a particular character performs inside the interaction. **Relationship → economy** remains the coupling rule.

---

## Locked brief (2026-08-11)

| Job | Pattern |
|-----|---------|
| **Reputation weather** | Continuous treatment (greeting tone, access, offers, withheld doors, rumor temperature, how the room shifts) without an explicit system line of “you did X → now mad.” Felt, not announced. |
| **Faction / household goal pressure** | Current high-priority goal of the local power center produces readable local residue (scarcity, urgency, hiring appetite, watchfulness, price shifts, road tension, hospitality temperature). War example: even after a war ends, settling takes time — supplies, labor, and mood remain affected. No faction sheet required. |
| **Fingers as interaction surface** | Players negotiate with the steward, quartermaster, patrol captain, priest, merchant factor, etc. The body is inferred from the fingers and the ripples. |
| **Players can become fingers or stones** | Hired, courted, blamed, protected, or used. Still the same surface. |
| **Altitude** | High = regional / institutional heartbeat. Pocket = the specific household / guild / village currently being touched. Same faction can carry both; players usually feel the pocket first. |
| **Opacity** | Some information does not reach the player until earned, DM-fed, or made obvious by the ripples. FP + free movement already enforces this. No mandatory faction HUD. |
| **Agency to ignore** | Players may ignore the weather if the effects are not worth the effort or simply feel boring. Open-world posture. No forced “faction turn.” |
