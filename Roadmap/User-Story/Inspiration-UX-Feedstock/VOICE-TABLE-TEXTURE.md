## Job (one line)

The table can hear who is speaking; the system transforms live voice; the DM still authors NPC speech; players may optionally mark IC.

---

## Why this pattern (not a desk)

Voice tooling answers a **table-presence** job: audible role separation at a shared live session. It sits beside session prep and collaborative agency — the DM shapes recurring NPC voices during prep or at the table; players opt in when they want IC distinction. It is **parallel** to World-cam (the DM live seat): possession handles *where the camera is*; voice handles *who the table hears*. Neither replaces the other.

It is **not** a rules surface, not combat chrome, and not Roleplay institutional weather (reputation tone, faction fingers, goal-pressure residue live elsewhere).

---

## Locked brief (2026-08-27)

| Job | Pattern |
|-----|---------|
| **DM NPC voice** | Free waveform manipulation, on-the-fly. Default or quick shape for one-offs. For recurring NPCs: shape once, save to that NPC; tool aims DM’s live voice at the saved target while speaking for them. DM remains primary author of NPC voice. |
| **Player IC signal** | Player sets their character’s voice once. Explicit “speak in character” control when they want the changer applied. Optional, never mandatory. |
| **Save model** | Saves attach to the NPC or PC record. One-offs stay ephemeral unless saved. No global “select from these voices” preset menu as the primary UX. |
| **Table presence** | Audible role separation and presence at the shared table. Not a production voice-acting mandate. Not a combat or rules pin. |

---

## Sense → Play → Outcome

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sense** | Live multiplayer blurs speaker identity — DM vs player, character vs player, distant vs near — especially in 3D. |
| **Play** | DM free-tunes or loads NPC-saved profile; player toggles IC; World-cam possession inherits actor voice (auto or one click). Defaults and speed keep one-offs frictionless. |
| **Outcome** | Recognizable recurring voices, optional player IC, no system dialogue authorship, no mandatory performance bar. |

## Session prep + live table

| Phase | Pattern |
|-------|---------|
| **Prep** | Recurring NPCs can receive a saved voice profile on their record before session — same artifact the live table uses. Fits `ux_dm_session_prep` without a separate “voice desk.” |
| **Live** | One-offs: default + on-the-fly waveform edit. Recurring: profile loads when DM speaks as that NPC or possesses them in World-cam. |
| **Ephemeral** | Unsaved tweaks die with the line unless the operator chooses to persist on the actor record. |

## Boundary vs Roleplay institutional weather

[[ROLEPLAY-SURFACE-TEXTURE]] covers **felt institutional pressure** — greeting tone, access, rumor temperature, faction goal residue. Voice changers do **not** implement that weather layer. They answer: *which mouth does the table hear right now?* Faction reputation may still change **what** NPCs say (DM-authored); voice tooling only changes **how the table hears the DM or player saying it**.
