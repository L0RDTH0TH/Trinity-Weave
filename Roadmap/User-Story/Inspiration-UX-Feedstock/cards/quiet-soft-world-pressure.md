# Soft world pressure inside quiet

**Job:** the world keeps moving while the table is on the road. Quiet waits; it does not shove.

## Pattern

Soft pressure the party can notice while simply traveling or camping:

- rumor
- weather shift
- distant sign
- NPC or patrol that appears because the world kept moving

Institutional weather (reputation, goal-pressure ripples) remains present. Class desks may still be opened. Neither is required for quiet to have texture.

**Player agency:** who to talk to, when to sleep, whether to push or linger, what small tasks to take up. No forced next beat. Players may ignore weather or pressure if it is not worth the effort.

## Refuse (required)

- Forced next combat / social / explore beat
- Class desk required for any quiet texture
- Mandatory authored beat every watch — or unread void with no residue path
