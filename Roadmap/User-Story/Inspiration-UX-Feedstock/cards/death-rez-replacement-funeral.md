# Death / rez / replacement PC — living-world exit and re-entry

**Job:** PC exit and re-entry as a **living-world event**, not silent token removal.

## Body states

Body / transformative states remain in [[conditions-felt-state-machine]]. This card owns the **party + institutional** lifecycle around exit and return.

## Resurrection

Rez carries **cost + heat** routed through pantheons, claim/provenance, and reputation weather. Not a free menu undo.

## Replacement PC

A new PC inherits **party-level residue** where narratively owed (reputation weather, claim heat, provisional-faction address, local institutional memory). Not a blank slate when the world already knows the party.

## Power-banded funeral / mourning

Same grammar as high-level monster ecology shifts:

| Band | Public scale |
|------|----------------|
| **High C** | Town / village |
| **High B** | City / region |
| **High A** | Kingdoms / larger institutional weather |

Lower impact stays personal for the party; higher impact is public and consequential.

## Joins

- [[conditions-felt-state-machine]]
- [[pantheons-institutional-faith]]
- Treasure / claim provenance membranes
- [[reputation-weather]]
- [[party-provisional-faction]]
- Living-world continuity / quiet-between residue paths

## Refuse (required)

- Silent token removal with no world event
- Rez with no cost or heat
- New PC with zero party residue when residue is narratively owed
