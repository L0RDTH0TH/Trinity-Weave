# Adventuring-day / rest — decision surface

**Job:** resource-day pressure as a **party decision surface** — not a hidden meter and not a pure menu.

## Preferred path

Above-table: **“should we rest?”** The party debates cost, risk, and opportunity in the fiction and at the table.

## DM gate (god window)

The **DM gate** actually opens the short/long rest window. Players feel control through the decision; the DM remains god over whether the window is available, partial, delayed, or blocked.

## Shared time budget

When the party takes the time, enemy AI and other agents receive the **same time budget**. What they do with it is their own (reposition, reinforce, leave, escalate, ignore). Rest always leaves **world residue** through that shared clock.

## Interrupts

Interrupting a rest is a **DM gate**, explicitly **rude**, and used **sparingly**. Not a default attrition lever.
