# Reputation weather

**Job:** the party is treated continuously by the place. Felt, not announced.

## Pattern

Continuous treatment without an explicit system line of “you did X → now mad”:

- greeting tone
- access / withheld doors
- offers
- unsolicited rumor temperature
- how the room shifts

**Opacity.** Some information does not reach the player until earned, DM-fed, or made obvious by the ripples. First-person + free movement already enforces this. No mandatory faction HUD.

**Agency to ignore.** Players may ignore the weather if the effects are not worth the effort or simply feel boring. Open-world posture. No forced “faction turn.”

## Coupling

Relationship → economy remains the coupling rule. Weather rewrites ordinary transactions and life options. Not a romance-amulet unlock.

## Refuse (required)

- Single visible reputation meter as the primary channel
- System narration of “they are mad because of X” as primary
- Mandatory faction HUD / inspect-to-know gate
- Class desk required to learn current treatment
- Romance / relationship as a single item unlock
