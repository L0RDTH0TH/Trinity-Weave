# Pantheons — institutional faith

**Job:** pantheons are **living institutional faith** — a source of faction weather, boons, pressure, identity, and sacred residue — not a mandatory god-picker or static lore encyclopedia.

## Institutional faith

Churches, cults, and sacred institutions function as **religious factions** with the divine as head. They operate inside the already-locked institutional weather, reputation, and knowledge-flow membranes.

## Optional engagement

Characters may leave faith completely blank at creation. Devout, nominal, opportunistic, or faithless stances are all valid and unsupported by system punishment for non-participation.

## Activity altitudes

| Altitude | Texture |
|----------|---------|
| **Fingers / institutional layer (default)** | Gods act in the world primarily through clergy, agents, temples, and institutions. Response is power-banded (low-band desecration → local response; higher-band sustained hostility or crusade → institutional mobilization at corresponding scale). Already covered by existing roleplay-permeable institutional weather. |
| **Personal / direct divine intervention** | Rare and **DM-gated**. Not automatic system behavior. |

## Oaths

RP / social / institutional weight for now. Binding oaths carry reputation, access, and narrative consequence through existing membranes. First-class mechanical oath system is **deferred**.

## Boons, pressure, and transformative links

Faith can source temporary benefits, institutional consequences, and thematic links to the already-locked Conditions transformative tier (blessings, curses, Seraph, Aegis-bound, etc.) without requiring those paths.

## Sacred space & residue

Temples, shrines, consecrated ground, and acts of desecration leave readable traces and can alter local institutional weather.

## Multi-pantheon support

Supported when seed-region size and diversity warrant it. Otherwise default to one primary pantheon plus local spirits/cults for simplicity. Exact seed-region size remains undefined; pattern notes the dependency.

## Knowledge & recognition

How much a character understands or is recognized by a faith is gated by backgrounds, knowledge skills, and prior interaction. No universal auto-knowledge.

## Refuse (required)

- Mandatory god selection at character creation
- Static lore encyclopedia players must memorize
- Automatic personal divine surveillance or intervention
- Treating every faith-using character as a direct employee of a god rather than a participant in an institution
- Wholesale import of a single published or real-world pantheon as project default
