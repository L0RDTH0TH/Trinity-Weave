# Traps — environmental agency

**Job:** traps are **environmental agency** — readable or discoverable features of the space that can be noticed, avoided, disarmed, triggered, or left as residue. Same family as `tool-use-as-presence` and quiet pressure.

## Discoverability & chrome

Gated by **investment** (Perception, Investigation, relevant tools/skills). Proper investment floats knowledge or chrome to the player — same pattern used for monster cohesion and push-to-run.

**Visibility spectrum:** some traps are shoddy/obvious, some subtle, some nearly invisible.

## Interaction

Avoid, trigger, disarm, or leave alone using existing movement, tools, and skill surfaces. **No** dedicated trap mini-game or separate resource economy.

## Player authorship

**First-class.** Players can set improvised or prepared traps as a common combat tactic (grab aggro → run the gauntlet → lure enemies into prepared space).

## Residue & continuity

Triggered, disarmed, or avoided traps leave readable traces (noise, marks, spent components). Can alert nearby creatures or change the space for later encounters.

## Placement logic

Density and lethality respect **power band** and **location** (dens, ruins, defended sites, wilderness) rather than random damage spikes.

## Refuse (required)

- Puzzle-minigame layer that pauses the world
- Pure invisible “roll or take damage” gates with no environmental texture
- Traps as the primary difficulty source instead of living creatures and space
- Universal auto-detect; discoverability is investment-gated
