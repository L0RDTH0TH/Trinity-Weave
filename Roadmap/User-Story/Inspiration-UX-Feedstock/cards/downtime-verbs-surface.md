# Downtime verbs — between-adventure option surface

**Job:** player-facing list of options for **available between-adventure time** — the most idle live-play should feel.

## Live-play shape

Above-table: **“what do you want to do?”** → DM records and resolves with the player and the system. Not a second sim loop the players must run.

## Verbs ride existing membranes

Examples (illustrative, not exhaustive): labor / site upkeep, hirelings staffing, commission conversations, knowledge-flow research, session-prep thread prep, pantheon / institutional contact, keep/forge presence, training via capacity membranes.

**Tools / languages** remain on the shared skill-readability surface; they appear here as **available actions**, not a new Tools desk.

## Boundary

[[quiet-surface-boundaries]]: quiet-between is **in-adventure**. Weeks/months and between-adventure verbs live here / living-world — not a forced quiet-between stretch.

## Refuse (required)

- Downtime as required sim / homework
- Downtime as pure menu with no resolution
- Tools as a new class of desk
