# Critical-node hierarchy

Nested, altitude-aware load-bearing nodes — people, bottleneck/unique resources, weighted locations/institutions, apex ecological presence.

## Degrees of fallout

Severity · time-to-stabilize · lasting consequence. Succession tools default to family trees and apprenticeship chains; some nodes stay irreplaceable by design.

## Civic vs apex

Most civic nodes remain essential **behind** power bands regardless of party strength. Apex ecological nodes are critical under rival pressure but are not “essential” civic anchors — presence can surface as event, especially in FP at lower bands.

## Refuse (required)

- Everything critical
- Player-facing destroy list
- Permanent irreplaceable collapse as only outcome
