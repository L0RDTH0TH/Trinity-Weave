# Language as barrier and bridge

**Job:** proficiency changes what can be understood, said, or read — without slamming the scene shut.

## Pattern

Barriers are **real**. Work-arounds exist and have **cost**:

- gesture
- intermediary
- magic
- time

Not a binary scene lock. Not a decoration that everyone ignores.

## Soft teaching

Languages can be learned or revealed through play. No mandatory training desk for every case.

## Refuse (required)

- Hard scene lock with no work-around
- Mandatory training montage
- Class desk required for languages to exist
- Everyone understands everything
