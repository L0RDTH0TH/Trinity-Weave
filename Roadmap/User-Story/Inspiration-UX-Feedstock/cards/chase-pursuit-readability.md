# Chase / pursuit — shared readability grammar

**Job:** sustained **multi-beat chase** (urban, wilderness, mounted) as a thin shared readability grammar between combat and travel.

## Thin surface

Not a full pillar. Not a second combat desk. Intermediate grammar only.

## Borrows

- Monster **push-to-run** + cohesion chromes (`monsters-habitat-actor-behavior`)
- Combat **enter/exit residue** (`combat-enter-exit-residue`)
- Quiet **travel loop** (`quiet-travel-camp-loop`)
- Traps / **environmental agency** (`traps-environmental-agency`)
- Travel modalities when relevant (`travel-modality-explicit`)

## Feel

Beats remain legible as pursuit pressure: distance, line-of-sight, fatigue, environmental hazards, interruption — without forcing a full initiative desk unless the chase becomes combat.

## Refuse (required)

- Chase collapsing into pure combat with no intermediate beats
- Chase collapsing into pure travel with no intermediate grammar
- Chase as a second combat desk or full pillar
