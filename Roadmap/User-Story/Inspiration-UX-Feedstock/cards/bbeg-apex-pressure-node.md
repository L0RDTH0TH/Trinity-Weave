# BBEGs — apex pressure nodes

**Job:** a BBEG is an **apex pressure node** — a person, institution, or ecological force that exerts sustained, escalating campaign-level pressure. It is **not** a scripted end-boss that waits for a final fight.

## Forms

| Form | Texture |
|------|---------|
| **Personal** | A powerful individual. |
| **Institutional** | A faction or faction finger actively pressing on the party or region. |
| **Ecological** | Monster stampede, spreading blight, marching armies, planar bleed, etc. |

Forms can hybridize or shift over time.

## Concurrency (power-banded)

| Band | Capacity |
|------|----------|
| **C** | Party can meaningfully contend with **one** apex node. |
| **B** | Capacity for **two** concurrent nodes. |
| **A** | Capacity for **up to three** (apex-of-apex load). |

## Off-screen continuity

The node continues to act, expand, react, and pursue its interests while the party is elsewhere. Removing lieutenants, resources, or local power balances produces visible responses.

## Readable escalation

Growth and reactions are legible through living-world signals: fingers activating, territory changing, institutional weather shifting, rumors, power-band consequences.

## Information control

Uses **existing** DM control surfaces for what enters rumors, knowledge-flow, and direct reveals. No special additional BBEG-reveal system required. Nature and identity are discoverable through ordinary play; timing and partial information remain under DM control.

## Party influence

Party actions can wound, delay, redirect, expose, accelerate, contain, or transform the threat. The pattern refuses railroads that ignore player choices until a climax.

## Resolution

Death (of the apex or of the party) is only one first-class pattern. Many situation-dependent victory and resolution conditions are valid: containment, banishment, institutional dismantling, transformation, co-option, turning the threat into a managed regional condition, negotiation, permanent exile, etc.

## Power-band awareness

Early pressure is local and limited. True apex weight arrives as the party’s reach and the threat’s reach converge. Low-band parties are not regularly expected to face full apex power.

## Refuse (required)

- Scripted end-boss that only becomes real when the party kicks in the final door
- Frozen threat that waits passively for the party’s arrival
- Mandatory final boss-fight-to-the-death as the only legitimate ending
- Design that requires every campaign to center a single named individual villain
