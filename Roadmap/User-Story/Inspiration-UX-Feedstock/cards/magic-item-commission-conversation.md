# Magic-item commission as RP conversation

**Job:** players commission magic work by talking to a finger of the local economy. That conversation is the surface.

## Pattern

Talk to the merchant, smith, temple workshop, independent magic-material dealer, or faction-affiliated store.

Independent vs faction-affiliated changes price, access, priority, and what is offered — same institutional-weather rules as other fingers.

## Boundary

| Who | Path |
|-----|------|
| Artificer subclass desk | Specialist personal / field-test loop |
| Everyone else | Commission conversation |
| Optional personal attempt | A Wizard or other character *might* try; not the ordinary path |

Skip / murderhobo apply to any optional packaging. World and NPCs keep operating.

## Refuse (required)

- Mandatory player crafting desk / factory-sim
- Ignoring faction / relationship on the same merchant
- Class desk required for commission to exist
