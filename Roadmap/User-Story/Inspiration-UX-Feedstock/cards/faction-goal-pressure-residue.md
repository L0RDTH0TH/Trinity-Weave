# Faction / household goal pressure

**Job:** the local power center’s current high-priority goal produces **readable local residue**. No faction sheet required.

## Pattern

Current goal or crisis shows up as place-weather the party can notice while simply existing there:

- scarcity
- urgency
- hiring appetite
- watchfulness
- price shifts
- road tension
- hospitality temperature

**War example:** even after a war ends, settling takes time — supplies, labor, and mood remain affected.

Players negotiate with **fingers** (steward, quartermaster, patrol captain, priest, merchant factor). The body is inferred from the fingers and the ripples.

## Agency

Players may ignore the pressure if it is not worth the effort or feels boring. No forced faction turn.

## Refuse (required)

- Mandatory faction turn every session
- Unread background with no residue path
- Campaign-map desk as the only way to feel high-altitude pressure
- Pocket mood replacing regional pressure (or the reverse)
