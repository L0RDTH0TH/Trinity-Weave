# Conditions — felt state machine

**Job:** temporary-to-semi-permanent **felt** states — embodied pressure with world teeth — not a flat condition taxonomy.

## Sibling modes

| Mode | Texture |
|------|---------|
| **Temporary / ordinary** | Short-to-medium pressure expected to resolve. |
| **Transformative / semi-permanent** | Longer, journey-shaping, identity-level changes. Follow **power-banded staged progression** — cannot skip to full form at low band. |

Both siblings share the same five families.

## Shared families

1. **Perception**
2. **Mobility / posture**
3. **Physical agency**
4. **Social agency**
5. **Ongoing harm / capacity**

## Transformative anchors (directional — native writing later)

| Register | Anchors |
|----------|---------|
| **Curse** | Lich · Lycanthrope · Vampire |
| **Neutral** | Primordial · Fey · Dragon |
| **Blessing** | Seraph · Fate-bender · Aegis-bound |

**Register:** the character **becomes the thing** (stronger than mild “touched” templates). Examples:

- **Dragon** — progressive transformation into a dragon; size only slightly larger than prior form; age / progression-based.
- **Fate-bender** — walking incarnation of fate.
- **Aegis-bound** — living divine shield.

## Environmental & survival pressure

- Frostbite / heat exposure ranks and similar environmental conditions live **here**.
- Resource-loss injuries (e.g. predator destroys food supplies) create a lingering **survival-deficit** state until resolved.
- Exploration environment fighting back is **conditional in nature** and stays inside this state machine.

## Mundane lingering injury (first-class)

Severe burns / lava trauma, amputation, multi-day high-level recovery, prosthetics, regeneration windows, permanent or long-recovery postural / mobility changes — alongside fantastical forms.

## Visibility

| Kind | Read |
|------|------|
| Physical states | Default-readable |
| Subtler states | Gated behind relevant passive / active checks (Perception, Insight, Investigation, etc.) + optional manual check with RP reason |

## Residue (middle path)

- Most temporary states clean up cleanly.
- A meaningful minority (public charm, high exhaustion, transformative forms, serious lingering injury, illegal magic, etc.) leave **social, postural, or institutional** residue that can persist and **travel** even if the party leaves the immediate scene.

## Recovery

Time, save, magic, rest, or active recovery that may route through existing tools, knowledge, or institutional channels. Transformative and serious injury paths have longer, more interesting routes.

## Player-facing communication

Embodied sensory / postural language + light directional chrome **first**; mechanical summary **secondary**.

| Direction | Example feel |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Stun** | Control lock + real-time world differential + crystalline border |
| **Poison** | Green vignette + actual poison effects beyond flat damage |

## Shared magic / environment readability (2026-08-14 — one-line lock)

Persistent magical terrain, concentration, zones, and residual magic weather are **first-class readable states / felt conditions** inside this machine. High relevant proficiency can float recognition (e.g. “this is a concentration effect — disrupt it”) via [[ability-checks-float-membrane]]. **No new card. No new physics system.**

## PC exit / re-entry

Body and transformative states stay here. Party-facing death / rez / replacement / power-banded funeral lives on [[death-rez-replacement-funeral]].

## Refuse (required)

- Pure numerical flags with no embodied or world texture
- HUD-centric primary experience
- Second complexity desk for condition upkeep
- Forcing every short condition into a full narrative event
- Wholesale import of any single system’s tables or skin (pattern extraction only)
