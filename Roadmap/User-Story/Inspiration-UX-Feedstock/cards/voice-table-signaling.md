# Voice changers as table / IC signaling

Collaborative-table texture. **Medium density** — intentionally light, not blocking.

## Job

At the shared table, everyone can **hear who is speaking**. The system transforms live audio in real time. The DM still **authors and delivers** NPC speech. Players may optionally signal in-character. This is **audible role separation and presence**, not a voice-acting product mandate.

## Pattern (locked brief 2026-08-27)

| Job | Pattern |
|-----|---------|
| **DM NPC voice** | Free waveform manipulation on the fly. Default or quick shape for one-offs. For recurring NPCs: shape once, **save to that NPC**; while the DM speaks for them, the tool **aims the DM’s live voice at the saved target**. DM remains primary author of NPC voice. |
| **Player IC signal** | Player sets their character’s voice once. Explicit **“speak in character”** control when they want the changer applied. Optional, never mandatory. |
| **Save model** | Saves attach to the **NPC or PC record**. One-offs stay ephemeral unless saved. No global “select from these voices” preset menu as the **primary** UX. |
| **Table presence** | Audible role separation and presence at the shared table. Not a production voice-acting mandate. Not a combat or rules pin. |

## Sense → Play → Outcome

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sense** | At a live table, overlapping voices blur who is the DM, who is a player, and which character is speaking — especially in 3D with distance and ambient noise. |
| **Play** | DM manipulates waveforms live or loads a saved profile on a recurring NPC. Player toggles IC when they want their character voice. World-cam possession picks up the same saved actor voice (auto or one click). |
| **Outcome** | The table hears role boundaries without the system writing dialogue or forcing performance. Throwaway lines stay fast; recurring characters stay recognizable. |

## NPC voice (DM detail)

- **One-off:** Improvise, default, or quick-tune. No setup gate.
- **Recurring:** Tune once → save on the NPC record → tool steers live DM speech toward that target on subsequent lines.
- **Speed:** Manipulation surface must stay fast enough for **live table** use; default always available.

## Player character voice

Same save-on-record model as NPCs. IC control is an **explicit opt-in** — never required to participate.

## World-cam / possession (parallel live seat)

World-cam is the DM’s **live seat** in the world. Voice is a **parallel table tool**, not a substitute for possession. When the DM possesses an NPC in World-cam, the saved voice profile for that actor should **auto-apply or be one click away** — skeleton-key posture, consistent with `forge-glow-dm-placement`.

## Boundary (Roleplay vs table signaling)

- **This card:** live audio transform for **who is speaking** at the table.
- **Not this card:** Roleplay institutional weather (faction greeting tone, reputation temperature, goal-pressure residue). See [[ROLEPLAY-SURFACE-TEXTURE]] — voice does **not** carry faction weather.
- **Not this card:** combat chrome, initiative, rules pins, class-expression desk.
- DM authors words; system authors **transform only**.

## Refuse (required)

- Global preset-picker as **primary** UX
- System-authored NPC dialogue replacing the DM
- Mandatory player voice changers
- Voice tooling as a combat or rules pin
- Game titles as `conceptual_pin`
