# Treasure & adventure rewards — content through membrane

**Job:** treasure is the **content** that flows through the already-locked claim, provenance, recovery, and economic surfaces. It is **not** a separate loot-table desk.

## Provenance & residual interest (heat)

Driven by three questions:

1. Would anyone **care** if this were stolen from them?
2. How uniquely **identifiable** is it?
3. Was it **theirs** (or their institution’s) in the past, and could they learn it has resurfaced?

Higher rarity / uniqueness raises both the likelihood that someone cares and the ease of identification. Common generic items carry almost no residual claim pressure; distinctive or notorious items can.

## DM control surface on prior-claim items

System flags relevant items for the DM (“belonged to Y / Y’s institution; they would care if they knew”).

- If DM does **not** trigger → no automatic information reaches the prior owner.
- If DM **does** trigger → choice of **delivery method only**: instant/direct injection, or through normal living-world channels and fingers.

Party can keep secrets. The only other path is the party themselves causing a leak through world interaction (e.g., identification by a connected finger, public display, sale into the wrong network).

## Reward forms

Coin, goods, art objects, materials, magic items, **information**, **favors**, and **site claims** are all first-class. Example of textured non-material reward: “do this and I will tell you where to find the important person from a PC’s backstory.”

## Hoard theming

Creature and location hoards are themed to their nature and place (dragon hoard reads as dragon, bandit cache as bandits, etc.). Reinforces habitat-actor logic.

## Appraisal & conversion

Some value is obvious; some requires knowledge, tools, contacts, or backgrounds to realize fully. Awkward or politically hot treasure creates logistics and social pressure.

## Residual pressure

Taking or displaying significant treasure can attract attention or claims once knowledge spreads. Leaving treasure can also have consequences.

## Power-band scaling

Reward significance tracks the band, risk, and location that produced it.

## Refuse (required)

- Classic open-chest → loot-table → abstract gold as the primary experience
- Weightless, consequence-free wealth that never attracts interest
- Automatic broadcast of ownership or recovery to prior owners
- Magic-item generation or shops that ignore provenance and living-world logic
- Treating information, favors, or site claims as second-class rewards
