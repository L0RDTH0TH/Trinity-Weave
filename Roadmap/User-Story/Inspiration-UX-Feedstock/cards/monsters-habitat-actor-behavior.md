# Monsters & behaviors — habitat actors

**Job:** creatures are **habitat actors** with readable behavior, not floating stat blocks. Signs, dens, patrol, and ecological role exist **before** combat.

## Behavioral modes (classical AI only — no LLM)

| Mode | Trigger | Texture |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Instinct** | Default for non-speech creatures | Simulates species / unique traits (wolves act like wolves; owlbear ≠ purple worm). Still modulated by the creature’s INT / WIS / CHA. |
| **Intelligent** | Speech capability | **Tactical layer** — threat assessment, focus prioritization, adaptation. Raises difficulty. Spoken for by DM / system voice. |

## NPC path

Speech-capable creatures may become **full-fledged NPCs** (social / continuity layer) fleshed by world lore and player interaction.

- **Intelligent mode** = tactical layer.
- **NPC status** = social / continuity layer.
- The two **coexist**. Party-side hirelings / allies that qualify use Intelligent mode on their combat turns.

## Mental profile readability

Discoverable via observation, knowledge checks, prior encounters. If character history + mental stats give reason to know, **float** the knowledge to the player as character knowledge.

## Group cohesion

Applicable to **pack / flight-style** groups (wolves, wyvern flight, etc.) — **not** ordinary humanoid groups.

- Degrades with losses; visibly affects coordination.
- Invested characters (Nature, Animal Handling, high Wisdom, etc.) receive a **dedicated cohesion chrome**.

## Push-to-run / run-readiness

**Separate** invested chrome tracking how close the creature or group is to deciding the fight is no longer worth it, and how readily further pressure will tip them into disengagement. Distinct from cohesion.

## Retreat & disengagement

Creature- and context-dependent:

- Territorial monsters rarely flee **inside** their den; more willing near territorial edge.
- Social structure and injury matter.

Primary legibility is **behavioral** (the enemy is leaving). Light chrome reinforcement for invested observers.

## Living-world vacuum

Removal of a sufficiently powerful beast triggers **localized** ecological / territorial rebalancing. Nature abhors a vacuum at **pocket scale** (lighter, local version of keystone logic).

## Residue & continuity

Tracks, dens, territorial markers, rumors, institutional memory, ecological shift after removal or avoidance.

## Refuse (required)

- LLM-driven enemy behavior or dialogue generation
- One-size-fits-all aggro AI
- Monster Manual content dump without behavioral / habitat logic
- Universal UI meters; cohesion and push-to-run are skill-gated
- Treating every speech-capable creature as a mini-BBEG by default
