# Hirelings — directed labor

**Job:** hirelings are **directed temporary or semi-permanent labor/agency** under the party. They expand what the party can attempt without becoming a second party or permanent minion swarm.

## Scaling

Capacity and quality scale with **power band** (C → B → A).

**Site ownership** (renovated castle, keep, fortified manor, etc.) creates **persistent staffing demand**. Failure to staff risks degradation, claim by others, reputational harm, or living-world consequences. The same membrane serves both adventuring extra hands and holding claimed sites.

## Cost & friction

Upkeep, morale, risk of loss, and **medium agenda leakage** are expected. Private motives can surface under stress, neglect, unpaid wages, or opportunity; RP delivered through the **DM**.

## Loyalty / morale visibility

Invested characters (Insight, relevant backgrounds, high CHA/WIS, etc.) receive chrome or floated knowledge on current loyalty/morale, following the same investment pattern used for monster cohesion and trap discoverability.

## Behavioral use

Combat-capable hirelings inherit the **Instinct / Intelligent** split already locked (Intelligent mode as tactical layer). Purely logistical or specialist hirelings run simpler task + loyalty logic. Role determines emphasis; no hard separate rule sets required.

## Party-side consistency

Intelligent-mode allies/hirelings use that tactical mode on their turns even while fighting **for** the party.

## Acquisition

Local pools, faction contacts, reputation, gold or favors. Tied to the living world rather than a global instant-hire menu.

## Residue

Dismissal, death, betrayal, or abandonment leaves social, reputational, or institutional traces. Loyalty and reputation with potential hireling pools are part of the living world.

## Hard constraint

**One party per campaign** remains intact. Hirelings stay subordinate; they must not become co-protagonists or dilute core-party spotlight.

## Refuse (required)

- Permanent companion characters that dilute the core party
- Zero-friction free helpers with no cost or risk
- Unlimited minion swarm or effective second party
- Global hireling marketplace disconnected from local reputation and living-world availability
- Treating every hireling as a full second PC with independent development desks
