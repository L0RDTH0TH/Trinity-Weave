# Active / passive skill assist

**Job:** one surface. One side is the deliberate action; the other is the continuous background radar.

## Active (deliberate)

Use of Investigation, Arcana, Nature, thieves’ tools, artisan tools, etc. **may** surface a simple low-chrome cue or leave world residue.

Examples:

| Assist | Cue |
|--------|-----|
| Nature | Green / yellow / red outline on animals (**already locked** on Combat — not a taxonomy HUD) |
| Investigation | Subtle emphasis on the incriminating page or loose board |
| Arcana | Read on the type of magic faced |

Not required to act. Failure / skip = no assist, not blindness.

## Passive (continuous floor)

Passive Perception, Passive Insight, other mental-stat passives. When the world’s opposing roll or state falls **below that floor**, the character receives the readability assist automatically.

Example: enemy stealth below the ranger’s Passive Perception → figure highlighted (or otherwise made legible) in the bush; surprise prevented. No declared check required.

Does **not** fire for everyone regardless of score.

## Fold

This is the ordinary availability of mental-stat / skill readability. `ux_mental_stat_interpretation` still owns cue-as-path-not-content. Do not grow a second mental-stat desk.

## Refuse (required)

- Pass/fail number with no readability
- Inspect-to-act gate / taxonomy HUD
- Passive assists for everyone
- Cues that spill the content
- Class desk required for the assist to exist
