# Commission formula and stock evaluation

**Job:** evaluate a commission against **current** local state — not a static catalog.

## Formula

Item base + magical reagents (what makes it magic) + time spent inscribing / binding the magical logic.

Evaluated from what the local economy and networks **actually have right now**.

## Economic questions the surface answers

- Is the base item in local stock?
- Cheaper or faster to import vs commission from the local smith?
- Did the player already make the base themselves with tools?
- How do scarcity, caravan status, and local production flex the answer?

## Realism bar

Background economy (caravans, production, scarcity) runs whether the party is present or not. Presence only **renders** the state (empty shelf, late shipment, inflated reagent price).

Avoid classic video-game unrealistic static economies.

Do **not** sim mundane paperwork ink. **Do** care about magical reagents and the binding work.

## Residue

Finished or failed work carries **provenance** into Inventory. Claim pressure follows existing network-routed rules. Knowledge of the object stays on the existing D&D ladder.

## Refuse (required)

- Static shop that ignores caravans / scarcity / local production
- Mundane clerical sim
- Formula as a player factory-sim
- Ignoring faction / relationship modifiers
