# Felt moment (pattern language)

A boss fight that is also a **family / herd / faction reckoning**. Climactic rolls, clever assists, near-death, and a killing blow that liberates a place — then immediate social fallout (who leads now, what power token was earned, how the crowd reacts). Combat never becomes abstract DPS; every turn carries identity and politics. Felt progression arrives as a trophy of authorship (a vestige-class payoff) and a change in standing, not a level banner.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Arena focus; camera/attention on the duel then the ring of witnesses. Soft framing keeps stakes personal. Pillar blend: combat resolves into roleplay without a loading screen.
