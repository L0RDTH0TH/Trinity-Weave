# Felt moment (pattern language)

A companion trusts an order’s **purge machine / sacred procedure**. It nearly kills them and plants doubt in the institution. Progression-as-identity: faith cracks; the player chooses whether to reinforce or abandon the dogma. Body horror agency — the “cure” is the wound.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Clinic / creche / shrine device space. Soft framing from the companion’s certainty then shock. Control is interrupt, pull them out, or watch.

## Experience noun candidates

- label: Cure that harms
  summary: Trusted procedure as betrayal of body and belief.
- label: Faith shatter at the machine
  summary: Identity progression via institutional doubt.
