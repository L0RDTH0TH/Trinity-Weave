# Felt moment (pattern language)

Final hours ask **who owns the ending**: sacrifice, transform, free, dominate. Companions react; combat may resolve the social choice or follow it. Party trust under cosmic stakes. Digital D&D’s version of “the table decides what kind of story this was.”

## Spatial / temporal / control

Climax arena / choice UI that still feels diegetic. Soft framing from companion pleas. Control is moral authorship before or instead of a pure DPS check.
