# Felt moment (pattern language)

Multiple apex threats hit a city at once. The party’s instincts say “stand and fight”; the fiction and the dice say **you will die**. Correct agency is evacuation, triage, and saving who you can — not clearing the encounter. Panic, argument, and shame coexist with skillful retreat. Models the rare UX truth: victory screens are not the only honest end of combat.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Wide disaster scale; attention splits across civilians and sky. Soft framing from devastation. Control feels stolen by the world until the players reclaim it through escape routes.
