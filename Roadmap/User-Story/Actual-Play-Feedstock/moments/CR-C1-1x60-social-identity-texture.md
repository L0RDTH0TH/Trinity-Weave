# Felt moment (pattern language)

Almost no combat. The session is **social ordeal**: returning to a childhood city / estranged parent / new half-family under diplomatic necessity. Titles get corrected; dignity is clawed back in a sentence; a child’s acceptance heals something combat never could. Side-deal mischief (theft for a guide) sits beside sincere family pain without breaking continuous fiction. This is the specimen for “table texture” and identity without a fight.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Palace / home interiors; etiquette as soft framing; who speaks first matters. Information is emotional more than tactical. Quiet between pillars is the whole product.
