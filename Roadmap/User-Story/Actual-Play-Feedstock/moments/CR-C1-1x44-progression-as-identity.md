# Felt moment (pattern language)

A delve finds a power object. A trap or mistake kills a party member instantly. The cost of return is not gold or a spell slot alone — someone offers a lasting **identity bargain** with a cosmic patron. The table’s silence, the sibling/partner panic, and the sense that “level” just meant *who you are forever* matter more than the resurrection math. Progression arrives as a scar and a role, not a toast notification.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Attention collapses onto one body and one bargain. The dungeon becomes a chapel. Soft framing from the world (omen, presence, acceptance) steers without removing player authorship of the vow. Exploration pillar freezes into roleplay; combat is absent or already over.
