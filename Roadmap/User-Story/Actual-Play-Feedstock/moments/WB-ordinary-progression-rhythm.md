# Felt moment (pattern language)

High-drama pacts (death→identity) are not the only progression. Most nights, **something small went up** and the table or camp reacts: a new cantrip joke, a companion noticing stronger hits, chrome that looks slightly more “yours,” a rest where people re-plan around new tools. The world and social layer acknowledge the change without a festival cutscene.

Digital D&D feel requires this **recurring rhythm**, not only mythic bargains — otherwise progression stays a sheet update.

## Spatial / temporal / control

Often at camp or post-fight quiet. Soft framing via companion line or world echo. Control: player can show off, hide, or immediately spend the new power.
