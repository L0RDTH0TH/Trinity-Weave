# Felt moment (pattern language)

Combat, exploration, and social are not hard mode switches. The **quiet between** — walking, camping, packing, banter, checking wounds — is intentional product, not dead air or a loading state. Continuous fiction means the same world, bodies, and relationships persist without resetting tone.

Name this as its own experience so pseudo-code cannot delete it into “press rest to refill.”

## Spatial / temporal / control

Camp, road, and post-fight linger. Soft framing from ambient world. Control: who to talk to, when to sleep, whether to linger.
