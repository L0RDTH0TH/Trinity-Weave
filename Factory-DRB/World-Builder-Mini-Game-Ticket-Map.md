## Framing (core-aligned)

The Prefer ladder ([[alpha0_stalberg_grid_kernel_r1]]) is the **craft shell** (hands). A finished Townscaper toy is the **wrong** done-state.

Genesis is a first-person table for friends. The world-builder mini-game is the long session **before** that: Dwarf Fortress–shaped world-gen, played with Townscaper hands. Campaigns attach later.

| Law | Statement |
|-----|-----------|
| Dual grid | How you **touch** the world — not what the world is (Hot Wheels ≠ Terrain3D) |
| Player authorship | Players **need not** author the first world (`does_not_mandate`). They **may** seed intents at Session 0 (`proposed → accepted`). DM owns world authority. |
| Tone | ToneProfile is **campaign**-bound at Session 0 in PMG; world create may hold a **default gen bias** that campaigns inherit or override. Do not hard-lock tone as world-only. |
| Natural terrain | Dual-grid facts **and** heightfield / noise–gradient–erosion natural-gen (stack intent) — not paint-only land/water. |
| Coast / UpdateFour | Classic “shared corner refresh” is long-term grammar; **2y** bits→Edge/Corner **KEEP-real**; **2z** strip-water **KEEP-real**; **2aa** Stage-6 land emit KEEP LIVE (parked F5). Place Full on owned KEEP (single-deck). Empty ClearDualSlot amber KEEP. |
| MapCam | Out of this mini-game. PMG MapCam for **play** adjudication is not killed by “no 2D world map product.” |

**This mini-game is done when** a DM can spend hours proposing, generating, reading, and hand-editing one persistent world, save/reload it, and that package already holds physical facts and world-facts a later campaign needs. **Not** when the tiles look nice.

**Still not this mini-game** (even when the list below is green): friends in FP, rules, combat, living tick, Terrain3D handoff scene.
